New Relic Released: Full Support

A companion to Drama Queen Pro that answers every Drama Queen post in guild chat with a supportive sentence, on your behalf. Drama Queen Pro 3.1 is out too.

Every crisis deserves a friend. Now every crisis gets one, automatically.

Full Support listens for Drama Queen posts in guild chat. When a guildmate announces that their car was repossessed, that the test came back positive, or that someone saved as "Kevin Gym" has a lot of heart emojis, Full Support waits a few seconds, like a person typing, and answers on your behalf with a supportive sentence that fits.

  • Pregnancy: "Sending you so much love, Vexa. One day at a time."
  • Court and jail: "Ugh Thorn, I'm so sorry. You don't deserve that stress."
  • Cheating: "I'm so sorry Vexa. Betrayal hurts. Take all the time you need."
  • Plus eviction, work, family, exes, and a supply of all-purpose comfort.

How it knows

Drama Queen Pro 3.1 sends a hidden signal with each post, so Full Support only answers drama and never ordinary guild chat. It never answers your own posts, supports each person at most once a minute, and pauses in combat. Type /fs off to stop, /fs test to see an example.

The same disclaimer applies

Full Support sounds sincere, because that is the joke. Guildmates may think you genuinely mean it, and the person you are "supporting" may not have agreed to the bit. Ask first, tell your guild, and switch it off the moment anyone asks. Read the Drama Queen disclaimer before you install either addon.

Download Full Support and the updated Drama Queen Pro 3.1 from the Addons page.

Hold light. And when your guildmate cannot, hold it for them, automatically, every five minutes.
