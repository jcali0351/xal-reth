The Litany of Begging

Ten thousand lines on the humility and complicated generosity of Xal'Reth. Please read the warning first.

Before you install

A Formal Warning to the Reader

The Litany is ten thousand lines long. It does not tell a story, does not build to anything, and cross-references itself until it loops. Nothing in it is dark, violent or disturbing. It is simply relentless, and relentless sameness is not good for everyone.

  • If repetitive, looping or circular text can stir up intrusive thoughts, spiralling anxiety or feeling unwell or unreal for you, please skip it with our blessing. Your wellbeing matters more than any scripture, real or fictional.
  • You do not need to read it. The addon performs it so nobody has to, and nobody should perform all of it.
  • If anything here ever leaves you feeling distressed or not yourself, stop, step away, and talk to someone you trust.

WHAT IT DOES

Perform It

Line by line, on a timer

Every line is an emote, every 3 to 60 seconds. /litany all (or the Perform Entire Litany button) posts the whole document from line 1 to line 10,000 without stopping, with a progress bar and time remaining.

Find Your Place

Titles, search and a saved place

Jump straight to any of the 200 Titles, search the text, or perform one line on demand. Your place is saved across logouts, and the performance pauses in combat and boss chat lockdown, then resumes on the same line.

The Notional Asker

Read someone into the Litany

Appoint your current target as the Notional Asker, and their name is read into the Litany as it goes, wherever the text calls for one who asks.

The Twenty Books

From Definitions to the Repeal of Nothing

Twenty Books of ten Titles each, from Of Definitions and Their Interpretation to Of Exceptions to Exceptions and Of the Final Provisions and the Repeal of Nothing.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install The Litany of Begging

  1. Download LitanyOfBegging.zip (243 KB).
  2. Extract the zip. Move the LitanyOfBegging folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  4. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  5. Type /litany to open the panel. You have been warned, lovingly.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Should I perform the whole thing?

No. At the default 12 seconds per line it takes about 33 hours. The option exists because the Litany demands completeness. Nobody should do this.

Does it contain real-world politics?

None at all. Despite the word Politics appearing nearby, it is satire of bureaucratic writing, not of any real religion, group or person.
