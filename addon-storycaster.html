StoryCaster

Write your own roleplay and perform it: /emotes on a timer, with real character animations.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 268 KB

Version 1.2.0Retail · MidnightFree

Paste a story, a speech or a whole scene, alone or as a duet, and StoryCaster turns it into /emote lines that play out on a timer while your character acts it out: a bow, a wave, a laugh, a kneel. Your words, your timing, no typing in the middle of the moment. Eighteen animated emote buttons on the panel cover everything in between.

WHAT IT DOES

The Lore Library

Ready-made roleplay for every character

Not sure what to say? Open Lore on the panel (or /story lore) and pick from three dropdowns: Class (all 13), Faction (Alliance, Horde or unaligned) and Race (all 25). Each one starts on your own character. Every choice is an original script of 50 lines or more, in scenes, with animations and a few lines said aloud: a paladin's vigil, a death knight remembering being alive, a goblin's first deal, a dracthyr's first sunrise.

Perform saves it to your scripts and plays it on your timer, Preview shows it in your own chat only, and Edit opens a copy in Write a Script to make it yours. /story lore blood elf performs one straight away. Target someone first and lines that mention them use their name.

Duets and Group Scenes

Everyone plays their part

Start a line with a character's name, like [Brannoc] draws his blade., and that line belongs to them. Press Play and your StoryCaster invites the others (through your party or raid, or a whisper), then runs the scene: each player's own StoryCaster performs their lines on cue, with their own animations. Everyone needs StoryCaster and presses Join.

Deliver, Scenes and Sharing

Live RP, on cue

/s, /y, /p, /ra and /g lines are spoken in that chat; /say and /yell wait for the Deliver button or key, because the game only allows them from a key press. Manual mode makes every line wait for Deliver, like a teleprompter. ## Act Two starts a scene you can jump to. Share copies a script as text for friends, and Edit Lines lets you start from, test, move or delete any line.

Placeholders and Pacing

Never the same twice

{target}, {name}, {class}, {race}, {zone}, {subzone} and {time} fill themselves in, and {smiles|grins|smirks} picks one at random. Auto pace gives longer lines more time. Ambient runs a quiet loop of small idle actions for standing RP, and five starter scripts show what is possible. Compact mode and key bindings (Key Bindings > AddOns > StoryCaster) keep it out of your way.

Never spam: on its own StoryCaster never posts faster than one line every 15 seconds, or more than 4 lines a minute, whatever the timer or a /pause says. Only your own Deliver presses go faster.

Write a Script

Paste it, it becomes emotes

Paste or type your roleplay. StoryCaster cuts it into emotes one per sentence, one per line, or packed as full as chat allows, splits anything too long for the 255-character limit, and removes your name, /e and numbering for you. A live count and a private Preview in Chat show exactly what will be said.

Macro-style lines work too: /pause 4 on its own line waits four seconds before the next emote, /bow or /wave plays the animation, and /s or /y lines become says, "..." emotes. A slash command is never posted as words.

Animations

Act it out

Write [bow], [wave] or [kneel] anywhere in a line and your character plays it as the line is posted; a line with only [nod] just plays the animation. With Auto animate on, lines that say bows, waves, laughs, cries or cheers animate on their own.

The Panel

Play, pause, timing

Play, Pause, Next and Stop, your saved scripts, a 15 to 60 second timer, random timing, Loop, Shuffle and a Test mode that shows the emotes only to you. It holds during combat and boss fights and picks up where it stopped.

Eighteen Buttons

Emotes on demand

Wave, Bow, Nod, Shake head, Talk, Point, Laugh, Chuckle, Cheer, Clap, Salute, Kneel, Cry, Shrug, Dance, Roar, Flex and Gasp, one click each, aimed at your target. {target} in a script becomes your target's name.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install StoryCaster

  1. Download StoryCaster.zip (268 KB).
  2. Playing WoW Forever? Download StoryCaster for Forever instead and move its StoryCaster folder into _forever_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Extract the zip. Move the StoryCaster folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  4. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  5. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  6. Type /story, press Write a Script, paste your scene and press Save and Perform.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Why does a second line appear when my character bows?

That is the game: every animated emote prints its own line ("YourName bows."). Turn animations off in scripts with /story animations if you only want your own words.

Can I rehearse without anyone seeing?

Yes. Test mode shows every emote only in your own chat window, and /story preview reads a whole script to you.

Can I edit a script later?

Yes. Type its name in the Write a Script window and press Load: every emote comes back one per line, with its [animation] tag.
