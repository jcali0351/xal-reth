StoryCaster

Write your own roleplay and perform it: /emotes on a timer, with real character animations.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 16 KB

Version 1.0.2Retail · MidnightFree

Paste a story, a speech or a whole scene, and StoryCaster turns it into /emote lines that play out on a timer while your character acts it out: a bow, a wave, a laugh, a kneel. Your words, your timing, no typing in the middle of the moment. Eighteen animated emote buttons on the panel cover everything in between.

WHAT IT DOES

Write a Script

Paste it, it becomes emotes

Paste or type your roleplay. StoryCaster cuts it into emotes one per sentence, one per line, or packed as full as chat allows, splits anything too long for the 255-character limit, and removes your name, /e and numbering for you. A live count and a private Preview in Chat show exactly what will be said.

Macro-style lines work too: /pause 4 on its own line waits four seconds before the next emote, /bow or /wave plays the animation, and /s or /y lines become says, "..." emotes. A slash command is never posted as words.

Animations

Act it out

Write [bow], [wave] or [kneel] anywhere in a line and your character plays it as the line is posted; a line with only [nod] just plays the animation. With Auto animate on, lines that say bows, waves, laughs, cries or cheers animate on their own.

The Panel

Play, pause, timing

Play, Pause, Next and Stop, your saved scripts, a 5 to 60 second timer, random timing, Loop, Shuffle and a Test mode that shows the emotes only to you. It holds during combat and boss fights and picks up where it stopped.

Eighteen Buttons

Emotes on demand

Wave, Bow, Nod, Shake head, Talk, Point, Laugh, Chuckle, Cheer, Clap, Salute, Kneel, Cry, Shrug, Dance, Roar, Flex and Gasp, one click each, aimed at your target. {target} in a script becomes your target's name.

Write in the third person, the way emotes read: the game puts your name first, so walks to the door. becomes YourName walks to the door.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install StoryCaster

  1. Download StoryCaster.zip (16 KB).
  2. Playing WoW Forever? Download StoryCaster for Forever instead and move its StoryCaster folder into _forever_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Extract the zip. Move the StoryCaster folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  4. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  5. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  6. Type /story, press Write a Script, paste your scene and press Save and Perform.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Why does a second line appear when my character bows?

That is the game: every animated emote prints its own line ("YourName bows."). Turn animations off in scripts with /story animations if you only want your own words.

Can I rehearse without anyone seeing?

Yes. Test mode shows every emote only in your own chat window, and /story preview reads a whole script to you.

Can I edit a script later?

Yes. Type its name in the Write a Script window and press Load: every emote comes back one per line, with its [animation] tag.
