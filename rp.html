    The manners of the Faces

    • Speak in character. Out-of-character notes belong on your profile's About page, or in our Discord.
    • Be kind, even when your character isn't. Rivalries are welcome; cruelty to the player behind the character is not. Report anything that crosses the line with the ⋯ on any post.
    • Pictures wait for an officer before anyone else sees them. Screenshots and art you made or may share only.
    • Your Battle.net account stays private. Only your characters on the guild roster are shown. Sign out anytime; the officers can remove anything on request through the contact page.
