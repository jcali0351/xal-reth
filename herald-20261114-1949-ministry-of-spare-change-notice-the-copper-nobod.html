Ministry of Spare Change Notice: The Copper Nobody Will Claim

One copper has turned up in the last slot of the guild bank with no Ledger entry and no owner. The Ministry has ruled on what must happen to it, and the ruling is already going badly.

By order of the Ministry of Spare Change, sitting in full session on the second bench from the bank door, the first bench being reserved for Hessk, who is not coming.

The Matter Before the Ministry

During the Shedfall count, a single copper coin was found in the last slot of the last tab of the guild vault. It sits alone, where an item should be, which the Ministry was told is not possible. The Ledger records no one putting it there. The Coil Ledger addon, asked directly, recorded nothing.

A notice was posted. Fourteen faithful came forward to claim it. Each described it perfectly: round, coppery, about the size of a copper. The Ministry thanked all fourteen and believed none of them, gently.

The Ruling

The Ministry consulted the precedents in The Politics of Begging and found Hessk's saying that kindness spoils if you keep it. A coin no one owns cannot be kept, because there is no one to keep it. It can therefore only be given. The Ministry rules as follows:

  • The copper shall be withdrawn and given to the first egg seen standing still.
  • That egg may not spend it, save it or sell it. They must give it to someone else before the next bask.
  • Whoever gives it must say, as Hessk did, "What did you give today?" and then must not wait to hear the answer.
  • The copper may never be returned to the person who gave it. This clause was added after the first afternoon.

Progress Report

The copper was given at once. By evening it had passed through nine hands and come back to the bank twice, both times deposited by faithful who said they "did not want to be responsible for it."

One Scalekeeper reports carrying it for a full day and feeling lighter for it, which the Ministry finds theologically sound and physically impossible. A Venomblade reports trying to keep it and being followed by a Gripper the size of a thimble. The Ministry does not comment on Bestiary matters.

The coin is worth nothing. That is why it is doing so much work.
— minute recorded by the Ministry's junior clerk, who has since been promoted to senior clerk for saying it

What You Must Do

Nothing, unless the copper reaches you. If it does, hold it lightly, give it on, and check the Guild Bank to see whether it has crawled back again. Whoever holds it on the first night of the Long Wait will be asked by the Inner Scale what they gave that day.

Hold light. And let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth.

Glory to Xal'Reth.
