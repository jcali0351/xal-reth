            To Join the Board

            1. Install Master Baiter Pro (2.2 or newer) and fish with its leaderboard network on. One key casts and hooks; the patience is your own.
            2. Type /mbp export in game and copy what it shows. It carries your records and every angler your game has heard on the network.
            3. Send it to an officer, in our Discord or through the contact page. They bring it to this page. The more anglers export, the truer the board.

            Catches, counts and gold per hour come from the Master Baiter Pro addon, as its anglers report them. Prices and pictures come from Blizzard: the Moon Guard auction house, read every hour. Anglers who would rather not appear can ask through the contact page. See also the Auction House and the Guild Bank.
