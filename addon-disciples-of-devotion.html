WHAT IT DOES

The Book

One hundred chapters

A panel with all 100 chapters down the left, each with its own hand-painted medallion, grouped into ten Books from the Book of Beginnings to the Book of the Open Palm, and the current verse on the right.

Read Aloud

Scripture on a timer

Press Play and the addon performs the chapter verse by verse as emotes, every 5 to 60 seconds or at random intervals. Or speak any single verse right now in Emote, Say, Party or Guild chat.

The Verse Engine

A million verses in a megabyte

After the hand-written verses, the Engine builds each remaining verse from hand-written pieces of the faith, arranged by fourteen sacred forms. No verse repeats within a chapter, and Devotion 42:7731 is the same verse on every computer, forever.

Daily Practice

Verse of the Day, bookmarks and search

One verse a day, the same for every faithful on the same date, shown when you log in. Bookmark the verses you love, search the hand-written scripture, and target someone to read their name into the verses that call on the faithful.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install Disciples of Devotion

  1. Download DisciplesOfDevotion.zip (1.1 MB).
  2. Extract the zip. Move the DisciplesOfDevotion folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  4. Fully restart World of Warcraft so the 100 chapter icons load.
  5. Type /dod. Your Verse of the Day appears when you log in.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Are all one million verses hand-written?

No. The 2,349 verses that open the chapters are the hand-written scripture of the Coil. The rest are built by the Verse Engine from hand-written pieces, so they read like scripture, never repeat within a chapter, and are the same on every computer.

Will this slow down my game?

No. The addon is about a megabyte and builds verses only when you read them.

Can my guild read together?

Yes. Agree on a chapter and verse, and everyone's addon shows the same text. One faithful reads aloud; the rest answer the Chant.

Why does Say only work for single verses?

World of Warcraft only lets addons speak in /say when you click. Perform This Verse uses your click, so Say works for single verses; timed reading always uses emotes.
