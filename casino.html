The house rules

  • One free spin a day for each Battle.net account, whichever of your guild characters you choose. The day turns at midnight UTC.
  • The spin is rolled by the sanctum's server, not your browser, so every result is fair and nobody can fake a win.
  • Tokens are not money and cannot be bought, sold or traded. Tickets are a way to say "I won"; the officers decide the prizes and hand them out at guild events.
  • Only characters on the guild roster can spin. Officers can void tickets from duplicate accounts or anyone who left the guild.
  • Hold light, and let go well. The Void keeps no grudges and no change.
