Bestiary of the Void: The Replayers, Who Live in Old Conversations

A new entry for the Wardens' Bestiary: the Replayers, small pale creatures that feed on conversations you keep having again in your head long after the other person has gone to bed. How to know them, and the gentle way to let them starve.

The Wardens of the Void have added a new page to the Bestiary. It was written at two in the morning, which the Wardens say is fitting, because that is when the creature is most often seen.

Description

The Replayer is a small, pale thing about the size of a hearthstone, with large ears and no mouth. It does not need a mouth. It feeds on conversations that are already over: the argument in guild chat from Tuesday, the thing you said at the summoning stone, the reply you thought of four hours too late. It sits on the pillow and plays them back to you, in order, and then plays them again with better lines for you.

A well-fed Replayer can perform both voices. The Wardens report one that did the other person's voice so well it began to win.

Habitat

Replayers nest in the quiet after logging off. They are rarely seen during raids, where there is no room, and never at the Basking Stones, where the warmth makes them drowsy. They are related to the Grippers, but where a Gripper clutches an object, the Replayer clutches a moment, which is harder to put down because it has no handle.

Signs of Infestation

  • You have drafted a whisper to someone you will not send it to.
  • You have rehearsed an apology so many times it has become a speech.
  • You remember the exact timestamp.
  • You have said "and another thing" aloud to an empty room.

Treatment

Do not argue with a Replayer. It has heard the conversation more times than you have and it knows all your best lines. Instead, the Wardens recommend the method from the Teachings: let the conversation finish. Let the other person have the last word, even in your head. A Replayer cannot feed on a conversation that has ended, the way a fire cannot feed on ash.

What is over is not waiting for you to win it.
— Sayings of the Warm Ridge

If the Replayer persists, say the greeting to it. "Hold light." It will not answer, having no mouth, but it will usually leave to look for someone still holding tightly. Sometimes you can then send the real whisper, the short kind one, and go to sleep.

For the rest of the creatures of the Void, see the Codex. Glory to Xal'Reth.
