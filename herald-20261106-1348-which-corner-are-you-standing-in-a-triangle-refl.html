Which Corner Are You Standing In? A Triangle Reflection for Mid-Shedfall

A small reflection on the three corners of the Sacred Triangle, the habit of the Three Pebbles, and why most of us spend Shedfall standing firmly in the wrong one.

Hold light, eggs. We are now halfway through Shedfall, the season when the Coil loosens its old skin and the guild bank, traditionally, does not. It is a good week to ask the oldest small question of the faith: which corner are you standing in?

The Sacred Triangle has three corners. Xal'Reth holds. Vyrenna gives. Nhalor releases. The Inner Scale teaches that a faithful life is not lived in one corner but walked around all three, slowly, the way a serpent goes around a world. Most of us, however, have found a corner we like and set up a chair in it.

The Three Pebbles

Among the older members of the Warm Ridge there is a quiet habit called the Three Pebbles. You carry three stones in a pocket: one dark for Xal'Reth, one bright for Vyrenna, one with a hole worn through it for Nhalor. At the end of the day you take out the pebble for the corner you actually stood in. Not the one you meant to stand in. The one you stood in.

Brother Tessk, who has kept the habit for eleven Shedfalls, reports that he has taken out the dark pebble on roughly every evening of his life, and once the bright one by accident while looking for his hearthstone. He calls this "a strong foundation". The Inner Scale calls it "a hoard with a prayer life".

How to Read Your Pebble

  • If you drew the dark pebble, you held well today. Good. Ask whether anything you held was ready to be put down. Xal'Reth holds the world, not the world's receipts.
  • If you drew the bright pebble, you gave. Vyrenna smiles, in moon-silver or gold depending on your week. Ask whether you gave from warmth or from fear of being thought cold. Hessk will ask you anyway.
  • If you drew the hollow pebble, you let something go. This is the rarest pebble. Nhalor rarely says thank you, but he has noticed. He notices everything. It is one of his four virtues.
Hold without giving, and you are a hoard. Give without releasing, and you are a debt. Release without holding, and you are a drifting skin.

A Small Practice for This Week

Before Shedfall ends, try to draw each pebble once. Hold something steadily: a raid spot, a promise, your temper in trade chat. Give something away without announcing it in guild. And release one thing you have been clutching, whether a grudge, a grey item, or the conviction that you were right about the boss positioning (you were, and it does not matter).

The Teachings say the Triangle points down because Xal'Reth chose the lowest place. Your corner is not your rank. It is only where your feet are tonight. Tomorrow they can be somewhere else.

If you have lost your pebbles, any three small things will do. If you have lost three small things, you are already practising Nhalor.
