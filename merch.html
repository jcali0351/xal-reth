COMING SOON

The Vault of the Coil is sealed. The forges beneath the world are lit. Touch the Eternal and see what stirs.

THE CALL

Faithful of the Coil: the day approaches when you may carry the Eternal into the world above.

For ages the cult has gathered in caves, crypts and slightly damp guild halls. We have begged with humility, given with complication and released with dignity. Now Xal'Reth asks something new of you: wear the Coil. Every shirt is a sermon. Every mug is an altar. Every pin is a small, shiny vow.

The Official Xal'Reth Merchandise collection is being forged now: tees, hoodies, mugs, enamel pins, posters and ceremonial robes, each one designed by the Inner Scale and blessed (at length) by the High Priestess. Quantities of the first run will be limited. The first to answer the call will be the first to wear it.

When the Vault opens, every purchase supports the guild: event prizes, raid nights, Discord upkeep and the eternal pursuit of more Rites. Xal'Reth asks nothing of you. That is why you will want to give.

Be first when the Vault opens

  1. 1
    Join the cult and the Discord. Members hear first, and the earliest believers get the first chance at the limited run. Join the Discord.
  2. 2
    Watch the Coil Herald. The launch will be announced in Official News the moment the Vault opens.
  3. 3
    Tell an officer what you want. Whisper a guild officer in game or post in the Discord with the piece you'd wear first. The most-requested items are forged first.
