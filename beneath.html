The Low Door

You went down instead of up. That is the whole teaching, really. Everything else is footnotes.

Beneath the pages of the sanctum there is a small room with a warm floor. The Inner Scale keeps it for people who find it. There is nothing in it except a chair, a candle that does not need lighting, and a note in the High Priestess's handwriting.

If you are reading this, you were patient, or curious, or you were told the address by someone in guild chat. All three are fine.

Here is the thing the Canon does not say out loud. He did not stay at the Open Door because he was strong. He stayed because he noticed that the world was cold and a little frightened, and he was warm, and long. That is all. Holding the world is not a feat. It is a kindness that went on for a very long time.

You can do a small version of it today. Hold something lightly. Give something away. Release something you have been gripping, even if it is only an argument about a loot roll.

Then go back up. The pages are better with you on them.

Hold light.
X.

This room is not in the menu, the map or the search. Please don't tell everyone. Tell one person.
