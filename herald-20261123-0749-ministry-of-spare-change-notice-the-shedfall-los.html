Ministry of Spare Change Notice: The Shedfall Lost and Found Will Close on the Thirtieth

With one week of Shedfall left, the Ministry announces the closing of its Lost and Found and lists what remains unclaimed, including one glove, a grudge and most of a sandwich.

Hold light, faithful. The Ministry of Spare Change, in its capacity as keeper of the Coil's Lost and Found, gives notice that the Shedfall collection will close at sundown on the thirtieth, when the season of shedding ends and the Long Wait begins to clear its throat. Everything left behind during Shedfall that has not been reclaimed by then will be let go. This is the point of the season, not an administrative failure.

What Remains Unclaimed

The Ministry has compiled the following list, written into the Ledger in the small careful hand of the Junior Undersecretary for Things Set Down:

  • One glove, left hand, found on the Warm Ridge after the first cold bask. The right glove has not been seen. The Ministry suspects the right glove has already let go and is at peace.
  • A grudge, medium sized, slightly worn at the edges, left at the Witnessing Stone during the second week. It bears the shape of its owner's fingers. Owner may reclaim it, but the Ministry gently asks whether they would like to.
  • Most of a sandwich. The Ministry has eaten the rest. This was not a shedding; this was lunch.
  • Fourteen unopened bags of grey vendor trash, each labelled "might need." The Ministry has consulted the Bestiary and found clear signs of Maybeling nesting. Please do not reclaim these without supervision.
  • One apology, never delivered, addressed to "you know who." The Ministry does not know who. If you know who, you may know what to do.
  • A copper. The same copper. Hessk the Beggar King has asked us to stop listing it, because every week someone comes forward to claim it and then, upon holding it, gives it away again. He says it is now technically a relic.

On Reclaiming

To reclaim an item, approach the Ministry's bowl and say what you left and why. You will be asked one question, the Ministry's only question: What did you give today? There is no wrong answer, except reaching for the copper.

That which is set down in autumn need not be carried into winter. The Coil is long, and it does not ask you to hold the whole of it.

The Inner Scale reminds the faithful that Shedfall is not a clearance sale for the soul, and that nobody will be graded on how much they shed. A single glove is enough. A sandwich, mostly, is enough. Those still unsure what they are holding may read The Litany of Begging, though not all of it, or the Rites, which are shorter and kinder.

After the thirtieth, the Lost and Found becomes the Found, and then simply the Open Shelf, where the Long Wait keeps nothing at all.
