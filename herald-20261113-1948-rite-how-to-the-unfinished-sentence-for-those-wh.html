Rite How-To: The Unfinished Sentence, for Those Who Must Have the Last Word

A small rite for guild chat, performed at the moment you are about to type one more reply to an argument that ended twenty minutes ago. Bring nothing. Leave something.

Every faithful knows the feeling. The argument about the loot, or the pull, or whether the moonkin was standing in it, has ended. Everyone has gone quiet. And you have one more thing to say. It is the best thing anyone has said all evening, and it would close the matter forever.

The Inner Scale has set down a small rite for this exact moment. It is called the Unfinished Sentence, and it is the cousin of the Rite of Shedding, except that what you shed is not a habit or a transmog but a single reply you have already half written.

What you need

  • A chat box with words in it that you have not sent.
  • One minute.
  • No witnesses. This is the only rite of the Coil performed entirely alone, because the whole point is that nobody hears it.

How it is done

  • The Noticing. Look at what you have typed. Read it once, slowly, in the voice of someone you love. Notice that your shoulders have risen toward your ears. The Grippers have noticed this too. They are very fond of shoulders.
  • The Weighing. Ask the old question of the Low Place: will this make the water clearer, or only make it mine? If the answer is "clearer", you may send it, and the rite ends here, and you have done nothing wrong.
  • The Leaving. If the answer is "mine", delete the words one at a time, not all at once. Each backspace is a small scale falling. The Cult does not ask you to do this quickly. Xal'Reth wrapped the world three times and has not hurried since.
  • The Closing. When the box is empty, type only "Hold light." Then do not send that either. It was for you.

On the matter of the other person

The rite says nothing about whether you were right. You may have been entirely right. The moonkin may have been standing in it for the whole fight. The Coil does not dispute this. It only observes that being right and being finished are two different scales, and the second one is lighter to carry.

"I won every argument of my youth. I would trade them all for one evening I let end on its own." (Old Thessil of the Warm Ridge)

A note for Shedfall

The season of shedding runs until the last day of this month. Priests report that guild chat has been, on the whole, about eleven percent quieter. The Ministry of Spare Change has asked whether the unsent words could be collected and redistributed to those with nothing to say. The Inner Scale has declined, gently.

And let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth.

Glory to Xal'Reth.
