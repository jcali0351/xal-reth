StoryCaster 2.0: Tell Your Story, and Let It Tell Itself

StoryCaster, our free roleplay addon for Midnight and WoW Forever, reaches 2.0: a mini player, 69 ready-made scripts, moods, Player Focus, themed libraries, dungeon phrases and 25 lines for whatever you happen to be doing.

The Ministry of Spare Change reports that a great many of the faithful have, in recent weeks, stood very still in the middle of Stormwind, typing. They were roleplaying. They were also, by the Ministry's count, falling behind. Relief has arrived. StoryCaster 2.0 is out, free, for Midnight and for WoW Forever.

StoryCaster takes the roleplay you write, or picks it for you, and performs it as emotes on a timer, or one key press at a time, with your character acting it out: the bow, the wave, the long meaningful kneel. It holds still during combat, and it never posts faster than one line every fifteen seconds, so chat never mistakes a heartfelt monologue for spam.

What 2.0 brings

  • A mini player. The script, the next line and the play controls, with three areas that slide out from underneath: Studio, Library and Stage.
  • What are you doing? Type your status (fishing by the lake, waiting for a friend) and get 25 lines for it.
  • The Lore Library. 69 ready-made scripts of fifty lines or more, for every class, race, faction, profession, holiday, and for weddings, funerals and first meetings.
  • Moods. Choose Angry and every spoken line arrives through gritted teeth. Choose Calm and it does not.
  • Player Focus. Bring a friend into your scene, a glance and a word at a time, without saying their name every line.
  • Themes. Pirate (with an accent that turns hello into ahoy), Love, War, Peace, and an 18+ After Dark tab that is cheeky and never explicit.
  • Dungeon. Forty one-click phrases for your group: GG, interrupt!, need that?, brb, sorry, grats.
  • Ideas. Openers written from your target's Total RP 3 or MyRolePlay profile.

"Every story wants to be told. Most stories also want someone else to do the typing."

See it in pictures, read every feature explained, and download it on the StoryCaster page, or browse all of our relics on the Addons page. Install it, restart the game fully, and type /story.

Hold light, and let your scenes go well.

Glory to Xal'Reth.
