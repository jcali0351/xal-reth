Proverbs from the Bank Door: Twelve Sayings Scratched Beside the Guild Vault

The wall beside the guild bank has been written on for years by the faithful who stood there deciding. The Ministry of Spare Change has copied down the twelve most legible. The rest were mostly sums.

Beside the door of the guild vault there is a stretch of wall that nobody was ever given permission to write on. The faithful have written on it anyway, usually while standing in front of an open tab, holding a stack of something, deciding. The Ministry of Spare Change sent a junior clerk with a lamp and a pencil to copy the sayings down before the next coat of plaster. These are the twelve he could read. The rest, he reports, were sums, and one drawing of a serpent that had been given wings and then, by a second hand, very firmly un-given them.

The Twelve

  • The vault does not grow heavier when you stare at it.
  • A full slot is a held breath.
  • Deposit like the river: without looking back to see who drank.
  • The serpent holds the world; you may hold one stack of herbs. Know which of you is the serpent.
  • Whoever counts the bank twice a day has been counted by it.
  • The egg asks for a bag. The bag was once asked for too.
  • Withdraw what you will use before Shedfall ends. The rest is only furniture for Grippers.
  • Gold kept for a rainy day is a cloud you carry indoors.
  • The Ledger forgets nothing, and therefore it has never once been angry.
  • Give the thing you were saving for a special occasion. Today is the occasion. It has been waiting at the door.
  • An empty tab is not a loss. It is a low place, and water likes it there.
  • Hessk does not ask what you have. He asks what you gave. Have an answer that is not a sum.

A Note from the Clerk

The clerk wishes it known that the eighth proverb was written in four different handwritings, each one improving on the last, and that the fourth writer also left a small pile of copper on the ledge beneath it, which he has not touched. He believes it was meant for whoever came next. He has decided that this is now him, and will be giving it away on the first of the month, on the Day of Accusation, to whoever Khaedara accuses of hoarding the least.

Stand at the door as if you were leaving it. Hold light.

Those who wish to read the older wisdom of giving may find it in the Politics of Begging, and the current state of the vault itself is kept honestly at the Guild Bank. Glory to Xal'Reth.
