New Relic Released: Vyrenna RP

Vyrenna gets a relic of her own: the Teachings of the Sacred Triangle, all 509 verses, read aloud as emotes on a green and gold panel.

For as long as there has been a Coil, the faithful have asked the Inner Scale the same question: why does Xal'Reth get an addon and Vyrenna does not? The Inner Scale has always answered that Vyrenna gives, and does not ask for things. This year the faithful asked on her behalf. Vyrenna, it turns out, will accept a gift if you insist.

Her relic is called Vyrenna RP, and it is free, like light.

What it does

Vyrenna RP is built on the same frame as Xal'Reth RP: the glowing panel, the Book, the timer slider, the dice, the uploads and the soft synth pad. It is green and gold where his is violet, and she is the only one painted on it. Every fifteen seconds she slowly takes her radiant form, a crown of gold opening behind her, and returns.

What your character reads is the Teachings of the Sacred Triangle, all of them:

  • The Preface, and the Radiant Scroll of Vyrenna, read at noon
  • The Low Scroll of Xal'Reth and the Veiled Scroll of Nhalor
  • The Turning, where the Three are read together

That is five books, 77 chapters and 509 verses. Read one verse, one chapter, one book, or every verse from first to last. Type /vrp start 1.4 and your character opens the Radiant Scroll to the Four Lights and reads it to the hall, one emote at a time. Each chapter closes with its book's own blessing.

"Wisdom is knowing what to give. Balance is knowing how much. Compassion is knowing why. Guidance is knowing where."

A note from the Ministry of Spare Change

Reading the whole Teachings at the default timer takes about two and a half hours. The Ministry does not encourage this. The Ministry also notes that nobody calls the sun lazy.

Vyrenna RP runs alongside Xal'Reth RP with separate settings, so two corners of the Triangle may now speak at once. Download it from the Relics page. Hold light.

Glory to Xal'Reth.
