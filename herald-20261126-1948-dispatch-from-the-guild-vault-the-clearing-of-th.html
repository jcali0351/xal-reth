Dispatch from the Guild Vault: The Clearing of the Untouched Tab

As Shedfall nears its last day, the officers opened the guild bank and asked of every slot the old question: has anyone touched this in a year? A report from the Clearing, the arguments, and the one item that stayed.

The Holy Calendar is plain about Shedfall: every faithful sheds one thing before witnesses, and the guild bank is cleared of anything no one has touched in a year. The first half of that custom is gentle. The second half causes arguments. The arguments are also shed. This week, with the season closing on the thirtieth, the officers opened the vault and the scribe sat beside them with the Ledger.

The Question at Each Slot

The rite is simple. An officer points at a slot. The scribe reads aloud when it was last moved. If the answer is "more than a year" or "the Ledger has no memory of it", the item is lifted out and laid on the long table, where anyone may speak for it before it goes.

Fourteen stacks of a cloth nobody could name were laid on the table. Nobody spoke for them.

Nine potions from a previous expansion were laid on the table. A Scalekeeper spoke for them at length, describing a raid night in which such potions might one day be needed. The officers listened politely to the whole speech. Then they asked when he had last raided with a potion from a previous expansion. He sat down.

The Arguments

There was one serious dispute, over a single grey stone from the fourth tab, slot sixty-three. Half the officers held that it was a vendor-trash rock. The other half held that it was a vendor-trash rock someone had put there on purpose, which is different. The debate ran for most of an hour. It was resolved when the Unpaid Intern, who had been silent, admitted that he had put it there two years ago to see whether anyone would notice. Everyone had noticed. Nobody had moved it. This was recorded in the Ledger as the most honest thing the vault has ever held.

A full tab is a held breath. Empty one slot and the whole bank exhales.

What Stayed

By custom one untouched thing may be kept, if the officers agree it is held lightly and not clutched. They kept the stone. It now sits alone in slot sixty-three with a note that reads: Notice me, then let me be. The Grippers, who had been seen nesting in the fourth tab since spring, are reported to have left in some disappointment.

The rest went to the vendor or to the Ministry of Spare Change, and the Guild Bank page now shows tabs with room in them, which several faithful have described as unsettling and then, a day later, as restful.

Shedfall has four days left. If you have not shed yet, the Warm Ridge is open and the witnesses are patient. Hold light, and let go well.
