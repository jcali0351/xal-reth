Drama Queen Pro 3.0: The Queen Detector

Drama Queen is now Drama Queen Pro, with a new Queen Detector that shows who else is running it and how many of you there are. Read the updated disclaimer first.

Drama Queen has a new name and a new question to answer: who else is doing this?

Drama Queen Pro 3.0 adds the Queen Detector. Every copy of the addon quietly checks in, and a new panel lists every other Drama Queen it can find: their name, whether they are in your guild, your group or out on the Drama Queen network, which version they run, how many posts they have made and when they were last seen. A notice appears in your chat the moment a new queen is detected. You are not alone. You were never alone.

What is new

  • Three tabs: Drama (the timer, the last post, Cause drama now, Say waiting line), Queen Detector, and Settings.
  • The Queen Detector: /dq queens opens it, /dq scan looks right now. Queens also check in every two minutes.
  • The Drama Queen network: a hidden addon channel that finds queens outside your guild and group. It stays out of your chat windows. Switch it off in Settings or with /dq network.
  • Settings tab: checkboxes for guild chat, emote, say, main voice, the network and new-queen notices.
  • A fix for the panel's timer display.

Read the disclaimer

The disclaimer has a new section. The Queen Detector shares your character name, version and post count with every other copy that can hear it, so anyone running Drama Queen Pro can see that you run it too. Everything else still applies: people will believe it, ask before you post, never use it to get anything, and switch it off the moment anyone asks. The full disclaimer is on the Addons page.

Download Drama Queen Pro from the Addons page, replace your old DramaQueen folder and fully restart the game. Look for "Drama Queen Pro 3.0 loaded" in your chat window.

The Coil sees every queen. Now, so do you.
