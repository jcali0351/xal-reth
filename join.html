Initiation

Join the Coil

The Coil is always recruiting. This is not suspicious. Please stop saying it is suspicious.

Step I

Find a Faithful

Look for the green robes, the gold trim, and the slow double blink. Blink twice back. They will know. Or ask in the Discord.

Step II

Take the Oath

Undergo the Rite of the First Scale and recite the Oath before witnesses. Feeling is encouraged. Typos are forgiven. Partially.

Step III

Receive Your Scale

You begin as a Hatchling. Learn the Tenets, answer the Chant, install the addons, and you will rise through the Turns.

THE OATH OF THE COIL
I swear upon the Coil Beneath,
by the Three Turns and the Unblinking Eye,
to shed what no longer serves me,
to wait as Xal'Reth waits,
to greet the egg and watch it closely,
to accuse the guilty and forgive the lagging,
to never pull while the healer drinks,
to answer the Chant whenever it is called,
and to keep the secrets of the Coil
from everyone, except those who ask.

WHAT THE COIL ASKS OF YOU

Very little, really

Show up. Be kind. Answer the Chant. Don't stand in the fire. Under the robes, the Cult is a group of friends who play together, help each other out, and roleplay a deadpan serpent cult because it is very, very funny.

All eggs are welcome

New players, returning players, altoholics, and the deeply unserious. You do not have to roleplay to be faithful, though you may find yourself saying "Glory to Xal'Reth" against your will within the week.

Enter the Coil's Discord

The faithful gather on Discord between Rites: event times, raid nights, guild news and an unreasonable number of serpent jokes. Eggs are welcome. Say "Glory to Xal'Reth" when you arrive.
