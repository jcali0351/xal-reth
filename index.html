Beneath the roots of the world, beneath the oldest stones, beneath even the lowest-ranked raiding guilds, something ancient is coiled. It is three turns around the heart of Azeroth. It has been waiting since before the first sunrise. It is cold-blooded, unblinking, and infinitely patient. It would like you to turn on your nameplates.

Its name is Xal'Reth, the Coil Beneath, Father of Scales, He Who Is Under. And the Cult of Xal'Reth is the small, devoted, extremely well-organized body of mortals who have heard him breathe.

We are not a secret society. A secret society would not have a website. We are simply an ancient order of the faithful who meet regularly, speak in unison, wear matching robes, refer to newcomers as "eggs," and keep a ledger of everyone who has ever wronged us. This is normal. Many faiths do this. Please stop writing it down.

THE FOUR PILLARS

Pillar I

The Coil Beneath

Xal'Reth lies wrapped three times around the heart of the world. Every earthquake is him adjusting his posture. Every lag spike is him yawning. Every server restart is him rolling over. The faithful do not complain about maintenance. Maintenance is holy.

Pillar II

The Shedding

All living things outgrow their skins. The faithful shed theirs deliberately: old habits, old grudges, old gear, and especially that transmog from 2014. What is shed is offered to the Void. What remains is closer to the Coil.

Pillar III

The Accusation

Every soul is guilty of something. This is not a judgment; it is an inventory. The Accusers merely say aloud what the Coil already knows. Usually it is loitering. Occasionally it is tailoring without a license.

Pillar IV

The Void

The cold dark beneath the Coil, where shed skins drift and the unworthy are banished. It has no flight paths, no mailbox, and no vendor. Rumors that it is just Silithus with the lights off remain unconfirmed.
