Who is Xal'Reth?

Beneath the roots of the world, beneath the oldest stones, beneath even the lowest-ranked raiding guilds, something ancient is coiled. It wraps three times around the heart of Azeroth. It has been there since before the first sunrise. It is cold-blooded, unblinking, and infinitely patient. It would like you to turn on your nameplates.

Its name is Xal'Reth, which in the oldest tongue of the Listeners means the one who is still here. He was not always patient. Once he was the Restless Serpent, who swam the dark devouring stars and was never full. Then, about to swallow a young world, he saw four things: his own shed skin, an empty egg, a dry riverbed, and a hatchling asleep in the sun. He closed his jaws, lay down beneath the world, and did not move for a night that lasted ten thousand years. When he opened his eye, he understood that hunger comes from gripping, and that nothing can be gripped forever.

He could have left. He could have stepped through the Open Door into perfect peace. Instead, he looked at the small, wanting, struggling creatures on the young world and made a vow: I will hold this world until every creature on it has learned to let go. He has been holding it ever since.

The Cult of Xal'Reth is the small, devoted, extremely well-organized body of mortals who have heard him breathe. We are not a secret society. A secret society would not have a website. We are simply an ancient order of the faithful who meet regularly, speak in unison, refer to newcomers as "eggs," keep a ledger of everyone who has ever wronged us, and are trying, quite sincerely, to loosen our grip on things. This is normal. Many faiths do this.

THE THREE TURNS

Three coils around the heart of the world

Xal'Reth wraps the world three times. Each coil holds something, and each is also a stage of the faithful's own journey.

The Outer Turn: Conduct

The coil that holds the land and the sea, and causes every earthquake when he shifts. For the faithful, it is how you behave in the world: greeting newcomers, repairing before raid, staying out of the fire.

The Middle Turn: Memory

The coil of the deep places and of dreams. For the faithful, it is what you carry: the verses learned by heart, the grudges set down, the names in the Ledger forgiven.

The Inner Turn: Stillness

The coil pressed against the heart of the world, where his own heart beats once a century. For the faithful, it is the last stage, reached by very few: total calm, and an open hand.

THE FOUR PILLARS

Pillar I

The Coil Beneath

Xal'Reth lies wrapped three times around the heart of the world. Every earthquake is him adjusting his posture. Every lag spike is him yawning. Every server restart is him rolling over. The faithful do not complain about maintenance. Maintenance is holy.

Pillar II

The Shedding

All living things outgrow their skins. The faithful shed theirs deliberately: old habits, old grudges, old gear, and especially that transmog from 2014. What is shed is offered to the Void. What remains is closer to the Coil.

Pillar III

The Accusation

Every soul is guilty of something. This is not a judgment; it is an inventory. The Accusers merely say aloud what the Coil already knows. Usually it is loitering. Occasionally it is tailoring without a license.

Pillar IV

The Void

The cold dark beneath the Coil, where shed skins drift and the unworthy are banished. It is not a hell. It is more of a basement. Rumors that it is just Silithus with the lights off remain unconfirmed.

FREQUENTLY HISSED QUESTIONS
Is the Cult of Xal'Reth a real religion?

No. It is a roleplay faith for World of Warcraft, written for fun, with a deadpan sense of humor. The Canon borrows, loosely and respectfully, from the spirit of real-world contemplative traditions, but Xal'Reth himself is entirely fictional. Please stop asking him for gold.

Do I have to roleplay to join?

No. Many of the faithful simply enjoy the community and type Glory to Xal'Reth when the mood strikes. Some of them become devout anyway. The Coil is patient.

Is the Cult a cult?

It is a guild with a god, a priesthood, scripture, an oath, a Void, a ledger of everyone who has wronged it, and a website. Draw your own conclusions. Then come bask with us.

Are the members actually reptiles?

The Coil neither confirms nor denies. Members are, however, encouraged to bask, and there is a statistically unusual fondness for warm rocks.

What is the addon for?

Xal'Reth RP performs the rites of the Coil as emotes on a timer: 10,100 rites, a glowing panel with Xal'Reth himself, a searchable Book, and your own uploaded rites. It is free on the Addons page.

Does Xal'Reth have wings?

No. See the Featherist Heresy in the Legends. Do not bring this up around the High Priestess.

What happens if I am banished to the Void?

Symbolically, everything. Practically, nothing. The Void is very boring, and most of the banished come back within the week, having shed whatever it was.
