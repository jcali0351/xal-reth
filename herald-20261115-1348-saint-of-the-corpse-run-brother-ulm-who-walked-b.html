Saint of the Corpse Run: Brother Ulm Who Walked Back Slowly

Every wipe ends with a long walk from the graveyard. Brother Ulm is the only saint of the Coil canonised for taking it at a stroll, and for what he noticed on the way.

The Coil keeps a short list of minor saints, and most of them earned their place by giving something away. Brother Ulm is different. He is remembered for walking. Specifically, he is remembered for walking back from the graveyard after a wipe without once using the word "again".

The Life

Ulm was a priest of modest gear and immodest patience. The chronicles of the Warm Ridge record that in a single evening his group fell eleven times to the same boss, each time at roughly the same percentage, each time because someone stood in the same purple thing. After every fall the raid released, appeared at the spirit healer, and sprinted. Ulm released, appeared at the spirit healer, and walked.

When asked by the raid leader why he was always last to arrive, he is recorded as saying: "The boss is not going anywhere. Neither, it turns out, are we."

He was, in fairness, also the healer, and so the pull could not begin without him. Scholars of the Inner Scale argue to this day about whether this was holiness or leverage. The Canon permits it to be both.

What He Saw on the Way

Ulm kept a little book of things noticed on corpse runs: a waterfall nobody had looked at in four expansions, a quest-giver who had been waiting since launch, a fox. His teaching was that a ghost who hurries sees only the door, but a ghost who strolls sees the world Xal'Reth is holding. He wrote it on the first page: the one who runs from the stone arrives at the same stone.

Release, rise, and do not rush the road;
the pull will wait, the purple will be there.
Hold light the grief of one more fall,
and let go well the blame.

His Feast and Observance

Brother Ulm has no fixed holy day. He is observed whenever a group wipes three times in a row, at which point any member may say "Ulm's walk", and the whole raid travels back at walking pace. Nobody types in chat during the walk. Arguing about who stood in what is deferred until everyone is alive, by which time it usually no longer matters, which was the point.

Eggs sometimes ask whether walking slowly costs the raid time. It does. About forty seconds. The Ministry of Spare Change has audited this and reports that the forty seconds were not going to be spent on anything better.

More rites of the low place are kept in the Rites, and other quiet heroes are gathered in the Legends. Hold light. Glory to Xal'Reth.
