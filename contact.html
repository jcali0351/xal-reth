Before you report a bug

Three small rites

  • Update: grab the newest version from the Addons page.
  • Restart fully: new art only loads on a full game restart, not a /reload.
  • Check the folder: it should sit directly inside Interface\AddOns, not a folder inside a folder.

Still broken? Tell us. Bug reports are a form of giving, and Hessk asks: what did you give today?

Response times

Patience, egg

Most messages get an answer within a few days. The Coil itself took ten thousand years to answer once, so this is considered fast.
