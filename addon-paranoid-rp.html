Paranoid RP

Target someone and slowly, publicly become convinced they are up to something.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 12 KB

Version 1.0.0Retail · Midnight 12.1Free

Your character glances, takes notes, strings up a conspiracy board, confronts your target, and finally tells them exactly what they think of their rotation. Every thought is an emote with a matching animation (point, gasp, cower, laugh, roar), and their name, class, race, guild and zone are filled in for you.

WHAT IT DOES

Act I

The Glance

It starts small. A look. A second look. Was that a smirk? Your character begins to notice things nobody else would.

Acts II and III

The Evidence and the Theory

Notes are taken. Timelines are drawn. The conspiracy board goes up, with red string, and the target's class, race, guild and zone are all pinned to it.

Acts IV and V

The Confrontation and the Trash Talk

Finally your character says it out loud, and then says what they really think of the target's gear and fire-standing. Never about the person, always about the rotation.

The Panel

Timing, animations and quiet mode

5 to 60 seconds between thoughts, or random timing. Start at any act, skip ahead, turn the animations off, or use /para quiet to keep the thoughts as an inner monologue only you can see. It pauses in combat.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install Paranoid RP

  1. Download ParanoidRP.zip (12 KB).
  2. Extract the zip. Move the ParanoidRP folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  4. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  5. Target a willing guildmate and type /para start.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Can I keep the thoughts to myself?

Yes. /para quiet shows the thoughts only in your own chat window, as an inner monologue.

Can I skip to the confrontation?

Yes. /para start 4 begins at Act IV, and /para skip jumps to the next act.
