Master Bait Forever: One-Key Fishing Arrives in WoW Forever

Master Baiter Pro has a twin for World of Warcraft: Forever. Same one key, auction prices, top five catches and live leaderboard, tuned for the Forever client.

World of Warcraft: Forever opened its gates this week, and its lakes are already full of patient people staring at floats. They deserve better tools. Master Bait Forever is Master Baiter Pro, rebuilt for the new world.

What carries over

  • One key casts and hooks, F by default, only while you fish.
  • Auction prices for every catch, with Blizzard's own fish icons and a haul counter that rolls up as you go.
  • Your top five catches on the panel, prices on hover.
  • A live leaderboard of every Forever angler your game can reach, on its own network.

What is tuned for Forever

  • Loads in the Forever client (interface 16001) and knows the original Fishing spell and its ranks.
  • Speaks both the modern and the original style of auction house, and reads Auctionator's Forever prices.
  • Scaled to a leaner economy: silver shows until you reach real gold, the jackpot starts at 5 gold, and the gold achievements are 100 and 1,000.

Install it in your _forever_ folder, not _retail_. Get it from the Addons page and type /mbf. Hold light.
