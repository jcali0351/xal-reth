Edgelord Pro 1.1: The Edge HUD

Edgelord Pro gets a panel worthy of its suffering: an RGB-lit gaming HUD with a glitching title, a terminal, and an Edge Level meter that climbs to Final Boss.

Edgelord Pro was released this morning wearing Brain Rotter Pro's old clothes. The Inner Scale has corrected this. Version 1.1 gives it a HUD of its own, built like the inside of a gaming rig at 2 a.m.

  • RGB light strip along the top and bottom that never stops cycling, over black glass and scanlines.
  • A glitching title in rage red and monitor cyan that jitters every time you post.
  • The Loadout: your ten categories down the left, from PC Mastery to Final Boss Energy.
  • The terminal: the next line types out after C:\EDGE> next_thought.exe, with a blinking cursor.
  • The Edge Level meter climbs as you post: Mildly Moody, Hood Up, Misunderstood, Lone Wolf, Shadow Walker, RGB Ascendant, Unbearable, Final Boss.
  • Big buttons: POST, SAY, AUTO-EDGE with a live countdown, and CHAOS. When a line is waiting for /say and the HUD is closed, a floating SAY IT button appears.
  • Fixed: the keyboard-clack sounds now actually clack.

Download 1.1 from the Edgelord Pro page, replace your old EdgelordPro folder and fully restart the game. Type /edge.
