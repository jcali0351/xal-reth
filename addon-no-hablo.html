No Hablo

A language filter for your own chat: one red button hides text chat written in Spanish.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 6 KB

Version 1.0Retail · MidnightFree

Busy trade chat on a bilingual realm, and you only read English? Click the red Block Spanish button and every line written in Spanish disappears from your chat windows. Anyone who writes in Spanish is muted for the rest of your session, so the conversation does not come back half-translated. Click it again and everything shows. It only changes your own screen.

Etiquette of the Coil: No Hablo is for your own reading comfort. Plenty of the faithful speak Spanish; be kind in chat, and never tell anyone you have filtered them.
WHAT IT DOES

The Red Button

Block Spanish

A small box you can drag anywhere. Click Block Spanish and it lights up as Blocking Spanish, with a count of the lines hidden and the players muted. Click again to switch it off. /nh hides or shows the box.

Every Text Chat

Sixteen kinds

Say, yell and emotes, trade, general and every other channel, whispers and Battle.net whispers, guild and officer, party, raid, raid warnings, instance chat and communities. Your own lines are never touched.

How It Knows

Words and spelling, weighed

It looks for words that are Spanish and not English (hola, gracias, alguien, quiero, porfa, jaja) and Spanish spelling (ñ, ¿, ¡, accents), and weighs them against the English in the line. "No problem amigo", "nada much" and "taco tuesday" stay put. /nh test shows what it would do with any line.

Only Your Screen

Nobody is reported

Nothing is sent anywhere, nobody is reported or put on your ignore list, and nobody knows you are using it. /nh list shows who is muted, /nh allow Name lets someone back, and /nh speakers hides only the Spanish lines instead of the whole speaker.

COMMANDS
CommandWhat it does
/nhShow or hide the No Hablo button (also /nohablo)
/nh on · offTurn the filter on or off (the red button does the same)
/nh speakersMute the whole speaker for the session, or hide only their Spanish lines
/nh listWho is muted right now, and how many of their lines were hidden
/nh allow NameLet one muted player back into your chat
/nh test some wordsCheck whether a line would be hidden

The setting is remembered between sessions; the list of muted players starts fresh each time you log in.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install No Hablo

  1. Download NoHablo.zip (6 KB).
  2. Playing WoW Forever? Download No Hablo for Forever instead and move its NoHablo folder into _forever_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Extract the zip. Move the NoHablo folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  4. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  5. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  6. Click the red Block Spanish button near the top of your screen.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Does anyone know I am filtering them?

No. It only changes what your own chat windows show. Nothing is sent, reported or added to your ignore list.

It hid an English line, or missed a Spanish one. Why?

It reads words and spelling, so a line with a quoted Spanish phrase ("mi casa es su casa") can be hidden, and very short slang can slip through. /nh allow Name lets a player back, and /nh test shows what it would do.

Some lines in a boss fight were not filtered.

In Midnight, Blizzard hides some chat text from addons during encounters. No Hablo leaves those lines exactly as they arrive.
