Edgelord Pro

Nobody understands you. Your frame rate, your RGB, your pain. Now everyone nearby gets to hear about it.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 469 KB

Version 1.1Retail · MidnightFree

The brooding cousin of Brain Rotter Pro. Every line is an edgy PC-gamer emote with a matching pose, and your rival's name is filled in for you: sighs darkly: "Anything under 144 frames is a slideshow, and I didn't come here for a PowerPoint." Case fans, frame rates, RGB, mechanical keyboards, energy drinks, damage meters and a tragic backstory nobody asked for. It is charming for about ten minutes. Then it is not. That is the point.

WHAT IT DOES

Ten Categories

From PC Mastery to Final Boss Energy

PC Mastery, Frame Rate Supremacy, RGB Is a Lifestyle, Mechanical Keyboard Liturgy, Brooding in the Shadows, Nobody Understands Me, Gamer Fuel, Meter Supremacy, Console Peasantry and Final Boss Energy. Every line is unique, and each category plays through before it repeats.

Auto-edge

A line every 20 seconds, forever

A speed slider from 1 second (unbearable) to 60 (brooding), with Chaos Mode for a random category every line. Auto-edge runs in emote, party, raid and guild chat, and pauses in combat and boss chat lockdowns.

Escalation

It gets worse on purpose

Tick Escalation and each auto line comes one second sooner than the last, down to the channel's minimum (3 seconds in emote, 10 in guild chat). Your guild will notice. That is the feature.

The Edge HUD

New in 1.1: a gaming rig of its own

A black-glass HUD with a live RGB light strip along the top and bottom, scanlines and a glitching red-and-cyan title. Your categories sit in a Loadout down the left, the next line types out in a terminal (C:\EDGE> next_thought.exe) with a blinking cursor, and an Edge Level meter climbs from Mildly Moody to Final Boss as you post. Big POST, SAY, AUTO-EDGE (with a live countdown) and CHAOS buttons, and a floating SAY IT button when a line is waiting and the HUD is closed.

The Panel

Hooded, headset on, RGB ring

A brooding hooded gamer whose headset ring cycles through every RGB color, with glowing eyes that blink when nobody is looking. Dramatic poses (kneel, roar, flex, salute, glare) every few lines, keyboard-clack and bass-drop sounds, and key bindings for instant edge.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install Edgelord Pro

  1. Download EdgelordPro.zip (469 KB).
  2. Extract the zip. Move the EdgelordPro folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  4. Fully restart World of Warcraft so the portrait and icons load.
  5. Type /edge, pick a category, and press EDGE. Please edge responsibly: aim it at willing guildmates, and switch it off when someone asks.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Is this related to Brain Rotter Pro?

It is built on the same engine, so it works the same way: categories down the left, a preview of the next line, EDGE, SAY, auto and Chaos. The lines, art, sounds and Escalation are all new.

How annoying does it get?

With Escalation on and the speed at 20 seconds, it reaches one line every 3 seconds in emote after about two minutes. We recommend not finding out in a raid.

Can I keep it to myself?

Yes. Set the channel to Local and the edge stays in your own chat window, where nobody can understand you.
