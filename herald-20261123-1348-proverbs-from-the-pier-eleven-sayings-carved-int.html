Proverbs from the Pier: Eleven Sayings Carved Into the Fishing Dock Posts

The faithful who fish have been carving into the dock posts for longer than anyone admits. The Anglers of the Coil have copied out the eleven that survived the weather. One was only a drawing of a fish, and has been left out.

Every fishing dock the faithful use for long enough grows writing. Nobody carves a proverb on purpose. One waits, the bobber does not move, the knife is already out for the bait, and something gets scratched into the post at elbow height. Over the seasons these scratchings become doctrine, or at least furniture.

The Anglers of the Coil walked the pier this Shedfall with a lantern and a patient scribe. Eleven sayings could still be read. They are set down here in the order the posts stand, from the shore outward.

The Eleven Posts

  • The first post: The water was here before your line. Be polite.
  • The second: A bobber that has not moved is still a bobber. So are you.
  • The third: Cast lightly. Hold lightly. Reel as if you expected nothing, and be pleased.
  • The fourth: The fish you lost grows larger every time you tell it. Let it stay the size it was.
  • The fifth: Xal'Reth lies beneath the lake as he lies beneath everything. Do not shout into it on his account.
  • The sixth: Whoever counts the catch out loud catches nothing more that hour.
  • The seventh: Full bags are a Gripper's nest. Sell the old boots.
  • The eighth: The deep water gives what it gives. The deeper wanting gives only cramp.
  • The ninth: If another angler has your spot, the lake has two shores.
  • The tenth: Patience is not waiting for the bite. Patience is not minding when it does not come.
  • The eleventh, at the very end of the pier: You may go home now.

A Note on the Missing Twelfth

There are twelve posts. The last one, half under the water, carries only a drawing of a fish with an expression the scribe described as "knowing". The Inner Scale debated whether a drawing may be a proverb. They concluded that it may, but that this one should be left where it is, since copying it would mean wading in, and nobody wanted to.

"Water is the lowest of the teachers and the most patient. It has never once asked whether you are any good at fishing."
— from the commentary of the Warm Ridge baskers

The faithful are reminded that the eleventh post is the most often ignored. The lake does not keep score, even if the Anglers' leaderboard does. For more on why the low places teach best, see the Teachings.

Hold light. And let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth.
