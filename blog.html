ABOUT THE DAILY COIL

The moon is calculated from the real lunar cycle, so the phase, illumination and countdowns are accurate for your date. The Weather Beneath is the Wardens' forecast for the Outer Turn of the Coil; press the button in today's post to see the real weather where you are (your browser will ask first, and nothing is stored). The color, number, scent and flavor are chosen by the date itself, so the whole guild shares them. The verse comes from the Disciples of Devotion Verse of the Day.
