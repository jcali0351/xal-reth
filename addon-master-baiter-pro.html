Master Baiter Pro

One key casts, the same key hooks, and every fish tells you exactly what it is worth.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 29 KB

Version 2.1Retail · MidnightFree

Master Baiter Pro turns fishing into one key and turns every fish into a number. Press the key to cast, press it again when the bobber splashes, and the catch pops up with its icon and its auction house price while a gold counter rolls up your haul. It is a helper, not a bot: you press every key, so it plays by Blizzard's rules. Formerly Coil Angler, rebuilt for every angler in Azeroth.

WHAT IT DOES

The One Key

Cast, hook, repeat

While a session runs, your chosen key (F by default) casts Fishing when you are idle. The moment your line is in the water, the same key becomes Interact, which reaches the bobber through the game's soft-interact targeting, so there is nothing to aim. Loot it and the key turns back into a cast. Outside a session your key is exactly what it always was.

Sessions begin when you press Start, type /mbp start, or simply cast Fishing. Loud splash mode turns sound effects up and music down while you fish, and gives every setting back when you stop.

Auction House Prices

Every fish, priced

Open the auction house and Master Baiter Pro quietly looks up the current lowest price of every fish you have caught, one at a time at the game's own pace, and remembers it. A Price my fish button sits on the auction house window too. If you run Auctionator or TSM it uses their prices between visits, and vendor value only until it knows better. /mbp prices lists them all.

The Haul

Gold you can watch grow

Every catch slides in as a toast with Blizzard's own icon for the fish and its price. A big gold counter rolls up your session haul, gold per hour races a meter against your best hour ever, and anything worth your jackpot amount (100 gold by default) sets off a golden burst and a raid-warning shout.

Top Five Catches

Icons with prices on hover

Your five most valuable catches sit on the panel as their item icons, framed in their quality color, with a count, a value and a bar showing their share of the haul. Hover one for its auction price each, how many you caught, the total and where the price came from. Switch between this session and all time. The summary card at the end of a session shows them too.

The Leaderboard

Compete with every angler

Every copy of Master Baiter Pro quietly finds the others, so the panel shows how many anglers are online right now. Open the leaderboard to rank everyone by fish caught, by most valuable catch or by this week's catches. Each row shows who is online or fishing this very moment, their count, and the icon and value of their best fish; hover it for the item and its price. Gold, silver and bronze for the top three, and your own row is highlighted.

It works over a hidden chat channel that carries only addon messages and never appears in your chat windows, so it reaches everyone on your realm and its connected realms. Players you have seen stay on the board (greyed out) when they log off. Prefer to stay hidden? /mbp network off.

A NOTE ON FAIR PLAY

Why one key and not none

Blizzard lets an addon decide what a button does, but every action has to start with a real key press. Master Baiter Pro lives entirely inside that rule. It never presses anything for you, never moves your character and never reads the game's memory. Its auction house lookups are the same searches you could type yourself. Fishing addons that work this way have been used for years.

What gets anglers banned is everything around it: an auto-clicker, a macro keyboard or a weight left on a key. Do not do that. Sit at the water, listen for the splash, press the key.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install Master Baiter Pro

  1. Download MasterBaiterPro.zip (29 KB).
  2. If you had Coil Angler installed, delete its CoilAngler folder. Master Baiter Pro replaces it.
  3. Extract the zip. Move the MasterBaiterPro folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  4. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  5. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  6. Type /mbp, walk to some water and press F. Then visit the auction house once so it can price your fish.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Will it get me banned?

It does nothing without your key press, the same way established fishing addons work. Using an auto-clicker, a macro keyboard or a weighted key to press it for you is botting, and that is what gets caught.

Why do my fish show vendor prices?

It has not seen them on the auction house yet. Open the auction house once and it prices everything you have caught. Auctionator or TSM fill in between visits.

The key does not loot the bobber.

Make sure soft interact is on (/mbp soft on) and face the water you cast into. If you already use F for something else, pick another key with /mbp key G.

Does it know when the fish bites?

No, and that is the point: the splash is yours to hear. Loud splash mode makes sure you do.

Who is on the leaderboard?

Everyone running Master Baiter Pro that your game can reach: players on your realm and its connected realms, plus your guild. Blizzard gives addons no way to talk across every realm at once, so someone on a distant realm won't show up. Numbers are reported by each player's own addon.

I had Coil Angler. Do my records carry over?

No. Master Baiter Pro starts a fresh record book. Delete the old CoilAngler folder so the two do not fight over your key.
