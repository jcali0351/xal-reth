New Relic Released: Brain Rotter Pro

Ten thousand lines of Xal'Reth brain rot, aimed at your target, your group or your guildies, with dances, chimes, a speed slider and Chaos Mode. Free to download now.

The Inner Scale has reviewed the following addon at length, in a dimly lit chamber, and has concluded that it cannot be stopped. It is therefore released.

Brain Rotter Pro is ten thousand lines of Xal'Reth brain rot. Choose a victim (your target, someone in your group, or a random online guildie) and the addon drops their name into a fresh line of nonsense with a matching emote. POV: Lord Xal'Reth just coiled around them in the mailbox line. They are deemed the frill lord. Lizard brain stocks are up. Somebody does the lizard griddy.

What is inside

  • 10,000 lines across ten categories: Skibidi Scripture, Sigma Lizard Grindset, Ohio Swamp Lore, Rizz of the Coil, Aura Farming, Goblin Brain Hours, Molt Mode, Main Character Hiss, Certified Gibberish and Final Form.
  • Actions and dances paired with every line: the Coil shuffle, aggressive mewing, the forbidden bawk, slow claps and falling asleep mid-sentence.
  • Chaos Mode, which shuffles categories every line, for those who have stopped needing structure.
  • A speed slider from 1 second (unhinged) to 60 seconds (chill), plus chimes and a key binding for instant Spew.
  • Channels: emote, say, party, raid, guild, or only your own chat window if you would like to rot in private.

How to begin

Download it from the Addons page, extract the zip into your Interface\AddOns folder, and type /rot. /rot spew fires one line. /rot auto keeps the rot flowing in party, raid, guild or your own window. In /say and /emote it is one keypress per line, as Blizzard requires and the Coil respects.

A word from the Inner Scale

The Coil holds all things, including this. Especially this.

Rot responsibly. Aim it at willing guildmates, not strangers in the Trading Post. If a guildie asks you to stop, stop: the serpent's first virtue is knowing when to let go, and the second is not getting the guild reported. Questions, victims and suggestions are welcome in our Discord.

Glory to Xal'Reth.
