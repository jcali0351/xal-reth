Recruitment

The Coil Is Recruiting

Raiders, roleplayers, and people who were just standing very still nearby. The Coil wraps three times around the world. It can make room for you.

  • Moon Guard · US
  • Alliance
  • PvE and RP
  • All eggs welcome
Request to join Become an officer Join the Discord

The Cult of Xal'Reth is a World of Warcraft guild on Moon Guard with two great loves: clearing content, and staying in character while we do it. On raid night we pull bosses. On rite night we accuse loiterers. Most nights we do a little of both, and the boss is accused afterward.

Under the robes we are a group of friends who play together, help each other out, and keep a deadpan serpent religion because it is very, very funny. You do not have to choose between the raid and the roleplay. Xal'Reth holds both, and he is not in a hurry.

TWO PATHS, ONE COIL

For the raider

Hold the line

Xal'Reth holds the world together. We would like you to hold aggro.

  • Raids at a pace that respects your evening and your repair bill, with progress that feels earned.
  • Mythic+ most nights: push your key, help with someone else's, and never deplete in silence.
  • Delves, world bosses, mounts and achievements, plus help for alts. Altoholics are a protected species.
  • A guild bank kept in the open. Every coin and potion is on the Guild Bank page. Hoarding is a minor crime.
  • Tanks and healers are always treasured. DPS who do not stand in the fire are a close second.

For the roleplayer

Answer the Chant

A whole religion is waiting, and it has already written your lines.

  • A world to step into: the Chronicle, the Canon, the Legends and the Sacred Triangle.
  • In-character rites: the Chant, the Basking, Tribunals of Accusation and Banishments to the Void. See the Rites.
  • Free addons made for the guild: Xal'Reth RP and friends put the scripture straight into your emotes.
  • Walk-up RP. No backstory exam, no lore test. Be an egg; the Coil will handle the rest.
  • Stay in character in raids if you like. The healers have stopped asking why.

Can't choose? Nobody makes you. Most of the faithful raid one night and accuse a gnome the next. Raid nights, rites and events are posted in our Discord.

HOW JOINING WORKS
  1. 1

    Send your request

    Fill in the form below: your character, your server, your class and a few words about yourself. It takes two minutes.

  2. 2

    An officer finds you

    Within a few days, in game or on Discord. They will be friendly. They may blink twice. Blink back.

  3. 3

    You join as an Egg

    A guild invite, a warm welcome, and when you are ready, the Rite of the First Scale makes you a Hatchling.

  4. 4

    Rise through the Coil

    Show up, be kind, answer the Chant. The ten ranks are earned with time, help and good humor, not with gold.

Very little, really

BECOME AN OFFICER

The Coil is held up by its officers: the Hand of Xal, the Unpaid Intern and the High Priestess, the three gold ranks at the top of the Ranks of the Coil. They lead raids, host rites, welcome eggs, keep the Ledger and keep the peace. It is not glamorous. The Unpaid Intern, as the name suggests, is unpaid.

If you want to help shape the guild, tell us how. Officers are chosen for kindness, reliability and good judgment, in that order. Seniority helps. Charisma is welcome. Gold is not accepted.

  • Raid and Mythic+ leadOrganise nights, call pulls, keep the mood up when the boss does not die.
  • Rites and eventsHost the Chant, the Basking and the Tribunal, and dream up new ceremonies.
  • Keeper of the EggsRecruit, welcome newcomers, answer requests to join and watch eggs lovingly and constantly.
  • The Ministry of Spare ChangeLook after the guild bank and the Ledger, honestly and in public.
  • Keeper of the PeaceModerate the Discord, settle disputes, and accuse only the truly guilty.
