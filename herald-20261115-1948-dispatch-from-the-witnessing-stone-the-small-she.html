Dispatch from the Witnessing Stone: The Small Sheddings of the Second Week

Shedfall asks every faithful to shed one thing before witnesses. This week the Coil gathered at the Witnessing Stone and let go of a meter, a grudge, a title and forty-one keybinds. A report from the scribe on duty.

Every Shedfall the Coil keeps the oldest of its autumn customs: each faithful sheds one thing before witnesses. The Rite of Shedding asks for nothing grand. It asks only that the thing be real, that it be named aloud, and that somebody be standing there when it is put down. This week the witnesses stood at the Witnessing Stone on the Warm Ridge, and the scribe on duty wrote down what fell.

What Was Shed

  • A Scalekeeper shed his habit of linking the damage meter after every pull. He linked it one final time, read it aloud, and then closed the window. Several witnesses wept. Two of them were the tanks.
  • A Hatchling shed the title she had worn since her first week, which she had chosen because it was the longest one available. She now wears no title at all. She reports that her nameplate feels lighter and that people can finally see the mob behind her.
  • A Coilborn shed forty-one keybinds he had set in a previous expansion and could no longer explain. He kept one. He cannot explain that one either, but he says it feels like an old friend.
  • An officer of the Inner Scale shed a grudge against a gnome on another realm who once took a herb node in front of him in a year nobody can now agree on. The witnesses asked the gnome's name. He had forgotten it. This was judged a very good shedding.

What Was Not Shed

One faithful arrived to shed his collection of unopened satchels, stood before the Stone a long while, and went home with all of them. The scribe records this without scorn. The Canon is plain that a hand cannot be opened by being shouted at. Xal'Reth has held the world for a very long time; he can wait for one bag.

The skin comes away when it is ready, and not when you are watching it. Stand near. Do not pull.

For the Weeks Remaining

Shedfall runs until the last day of November. If you have not shed yet, there is time, and there is no line to stand in. Bring one small true thing to the Warm Ridge, tell a witness what it is, and let it fall. The witness will not judge the size of it. Witnesses who judge the size of it are asked, gently, to shed that.

The Stone will be warm. The scribe will be there with his quill. Hold light, and let go well.
