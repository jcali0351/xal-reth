How to Run a Guild Reading Night with Disciples of Devotion

Pick a chapter, gather the faithful, and read the scripture of Xal'Reth aloud together. A step-by-step guide for officers and anyone brave enough to host.

AddonsOctober 26, 2026the 26th day of Shedfall, Year 2 of the Restoration2 min read

Posted by the Wardens of the Void in the Daily Coil

Because every verse in Disciples of Devotion is built from its number, the whole guild sees the same text. That makes a reading night easy to run. Here is how.

Before the night

  • Choose a chapter. For a first night, Chapter 3 (The Four Sights), Chapter 43 (The Chant) or Chapter 64 (Hessk the Beggar King) are short, story-driven and well loved. Browse them in the live reader.
  • Choose a place. Somewhere quiet, warm and a little scenic. A sunny ridge, a ruined tower, the steps of an inn. Avoid capital-city traffic unless you want an audience.
  • Choose a reader. One person reads; everyone else listens and responds. Rotate readers between chapters.
  • Announce it. Post the chapter and verse range in guild chat and on Discord: Tonight, Devotion 3:1 to 3:20, at the Warm Ridge.

On the night

  1. Gather. The reader opens with the Prayer of the Triangle or a single Glory to Xal'Reth.
  2. The reader types /dod play 3:1, sets the interval to 20 or 25 seconds, and ticks Stop after hand-written verses so the reading ends where the scripture does.
  3. Listeners respond in emote between verses: a bow, a nod, a quiet So it is coiled. Keep chat light so the verses stand out.
  4. At the end, the reader calls the Chant. Everyone answers.

After the night

Ask everyone to bookmark the verse that stayed with them (/dod mark) and share it in guild chat. Pick the next chapter. Over a season, a guild can read the whole of the hand-written scripture together, two thousand verses, one chapter at a time.

Tips

If combat breaks out, the addon pauses on its own and resumes on the same verse. If someone joins late, they can type /dod goto with the current verse number to catch up. If the reader's target is on a newcomer, the verses that address the faithful will speak to them by name. That is the best part.
