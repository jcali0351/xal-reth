Bestiary of the Void: The Glancelings, Who Feed on Checking Again

A small creature of the Void that lives in the half-second before you look again: at the auction house, at the roster, at the whisper that has not come. Field notes, signs of infestation, and the one remedy the Wardens trust.

The Wardens of the Void file the Glanceling among the lesser kin of the Grippers. It does not cling. It does not wrap. It simply waits in the half-second before you look again, and it eats that half-second, and it is hungry for the next one.

Appearance

No faithful has seen a Glanceling directly, because it is always just behind whatever you were checking. Those who have glimpsed one at the edge of the eye describe something the size of a copper coin, round, lidless, and faintly pleased. It has no mouth. Brother Tavik of the Warm Ridge insists it has a very small mouth and that he heard it chew. The Inner Scale has not ruled on the mouth.

Habitat

Glancelings nest wherever a thing might have changed since a moment ago:

  • The auction house, between one search and the identical search.
  • The guild roster, where a faithful checks whether a certain friend has logged in, then checks again, as if the first check might have been lying.
  • The mailbox, especially when nothing was sent.
  • The whisper window of anyone who has just written "so anyway" and is waiting for a reply.

Signs of infestation

The afflicted faithful refreshes a page that refreshes itself. They open their bags to confirm the item is still there. They read the same guild message of the day four times and could not tell you what it says. Their thumb develops a small, polished groove. In advanced cases, they begin checking whether they have checked.

The serpent beneath the world does not look up to see whether the world is still there. He feels it, and is content. — attributed to the Low Scroll, recited at the Ridge

The remedy

The Wardens have tried many things. Swatting fails; the Glanceling is behind the swat. Looking very quickly fails; it is behind that too. The only remedy the Wardens trust is called the Second Breath. When you feel the pull to look again, take one breath and ask: what would change if I knew right now? If the answer is nothing, take a second breath and do not look. The Glanceling, denied its half-second, grows thin and wanders off to the next faithful, who is usually standing by the mailbox.

This is not a grand practice. It is the smallest form of letting go there is, and that is why it works. See also the Teachings on patience and the low place.

Hold light, and look once. Glory to Xal'Reth.
