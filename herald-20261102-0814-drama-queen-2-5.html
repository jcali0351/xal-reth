Drama Queen 2.5: Now in Emote and Say

Drama Queen now acts out its drama as an emote and says it out loud, as well as posting to guild chat. Read the updated disclaimer before you update.

Drama Queen has been updated to version 2.5. It no longer keeps its troubles to guild chat.

  • Emote: every five minutes your character now acts the drama out as well. Stares at their phone. "I think I'm pregnant." Pinches the bridge of their nose. "Kevin Gym is my cousin Kayla."
  • Say: the line is spoken aloud in /say. Inside dungeons and raids this happens automatically. Out in the world, Blizzard requires a real key press for /say, so a glowing Say it button appears with the line ready. Click it, type /dq say, or bind a key under Key Bindings > AddOns. Right-click the button to skip a line.
  • Each channel can be switched off: /dq guild, /dq emote and /dq saymode.

The disclaimer has grown

Emote and /say are public. Everyone near you can see them, including strangers and players outside the guild, and public drama is far more likely to be reported than guild chat. Turn emote and say off in busy cities and in groups with people you don't know, and stop at once if anyone objects. The full, updated disclaimer is on the Addons page.

Download version 2.5 from the Addons page, replace your old DramaQueen folder and fully restart the game. Look for "Drama Queen 2.5 (guild, emote and say) loaded" in your chat window.

What is said in the guild hall echoes in the street. Speak accordingly.
