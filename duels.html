    The Web of Grievances

    Every line is a pair who have duelled; the thicker, the more often. Point at a duelist to light their rivals; choose one to open their page.

    Recording a duel

    Tell an officer who won, who lost and where. They write it in the ledger, and the board updates within a few minutes. Duels are fought in the open world and with consent, in keeping with the Seven Tenets. Gloating is recorded separately.
