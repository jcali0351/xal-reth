Master Bait Forever

One key casts, the same key hooks, and every fish tells you what it is worth, now in World of Warcraft: Forever.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 30 KB

Version 1.0WoW ForeverFree

Master Bait Forever is Master Baiter Pro rebuilt for World of Warcraft: Forever. Press one key to cast, press it again on the splash, and every catch pops up with its icon and auction price while the haul counter rolls up. It is a helper, not a bot: you press every key.

WHAT IT DOES

Built for Forever

Same addon, new world

It loads in the Forever client (interface 16001), recognises the original Fishing spell and its ranks, and scales everything to Forever's leaner economy: the haul shows silver until you are into real gold, the jackpot starts at 5 gold, and the gold achievements are 100 and 1,000 gold.

Auction House Prices

Either kind of auction house

Open the auction house and it prices every fish you have caught. It understands both the modern item-key auction house and the original browse-and-page one, so it works whichever Forever uses, and it reads Auctionator's Forever prices between visits. Vendor value fills in until then.

The One Key

Cast, hook, repeat

Your chosen key (F by default) casts Fishing while you are idle and becomes Interact while your line is in the water, reaching the bobber through soft interact. Loud splash mode turns effects up and music down, and gives everything back when you stop.

Top Five and the Leaderboard

Icons, prices, rivals

Your five most valuable catches sit on the panel as their item icons with prices on hover. The leaderboard finds every other Master Bait Forever angler your game can reach, shows how many are online, and ranks them by fish caught, best catch or this week. It runs on its own Forever network, separate from Master Baiter Pro.

A NOTE ON FAIR PLAY

One key, never zero

Every action still starts with your own key press, exactly like Master Baiter Pro. It never presses anything for you. An auto-clicker or a weighted key is botting, and that is what gets accounts banned.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install Master Bait Forever

  1. Download MasterBaitForever.zip (30 KB).
  2. This is the Forever version. For Midnight, get Master Baiter Pro instead; you can run each in its own game.
  3. Extract the zip. Move the MasterBaitForever folder into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns.
  4. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  5. Restart World of Warcraft: Forever or type /reload.
  6. Type /mbf, walk to some water and press F. Visit the auction house once so it can price your fish.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Is this different from Master Baiter Pro?

Same features, tuned for Forever: interface 16001, the original Fishing spell, both kinds of auction house, a smaller gold scale and its own leaderboard network.

It says out of date.

Make sure you put it in the _forever_ folder, not _retail_ or _classic_era_. If a Forever patch raises the interface number, tick Load out of date AddOns on the character screen until we update it.

Why do my fish show vendor prices?

It has not seen them on the auction house yet. Open the auction house once, or install Auctionator's Forever version.

Will it get me banned?

It does nothing without your key press, like established fishing addons. Never put a clicker or a weight on the key.
