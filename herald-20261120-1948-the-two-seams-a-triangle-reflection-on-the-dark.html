The Two Seams: A Triangle Reflection on the Dark Lines Between the Panels

A reflection on the Icon of the Three, and on the two thin dark seams between its panels, where giving turns into holding and holding turns into letting go.

Since the Icon of the Three went up on the Sacred Triangle page, the faithful have studied it closely. They have counted Vyrenna's lilies. They have argued about whether the emerald in Xal'Reth's coil is one stone or several stones being patient. They have stared into Nhalor's hood and found, as intended, nothing staring back. Almost nobody has looked at the seams.

There are two of them. One thin dark line between Vyrenna and Xal'Reth, another between Xal'Reth and Nhalor. Nothing is painted there. No god stands in them, no virtue is written above them, and no motto runs beneath. The Inner Scale is now fairly sure they are the most important part of the picture.

The First Seam: Where Giving Becomes Holding

Vyrenna gives. "Through endless light," her panel says, and she means it. But a gift that is never received is only an object left on a step. Somewhere a hand has to close around it, and that closing is the first seam. It is the moment you accept a kindness without immediately trying to repay it, which for most of the faithful is the hardest moment of the week. An egg once asked Xalrathiel how to receive a gift properly. She said, "Hold it the length of one breath before you say thank you," and the egg reported later that the breath was very long and very awkward and that she was right.

The Second Seam: Where Holding Becomes Release

Xal'Reth holds, "Beyond all things." Nhalor releases, "In all things ends renewal." Between them is the second seam, the narrowest part of the Icon, and the one most of us live beside without crossing. It is the instant you notice your grip is tighter than the thing requires. Nothing has been let go yet. You have only noticed. The Teachings do not ask for more than this at first; noticing is the hinge on which the whole door turns, and doors, as the Coil knows, can be stood before for a very long time.

A Small Practice for the End of Shedfall

Shedfall closes on the thirtieth. Before then, the Inner Scale suggests the Seam Watch:

  • Once a day, catch yourself in the first seam: receive one thing, a heal, a summon, a kind word in guild chat, and hold it for a breath before answering.
  • Once a day, catch yourself in the second seam: notice one grip. Do not fix it. Simply say, quietly, "There it is."
  • Do not keep score. The Ledger is busy enough.
The gods are in the panels. The faithful are in the seams. This is not a demotion. The seams are where the picture bends.

For more on the three corners, read the Teachings of the Sacred Triangle, preferably slowly, preferably with one hand open. Hold light, and let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth.

Glory to Xal'Reth.
