Every Text of the Faith

The Library of the Coil

Scriptures, histories, legends and references, gathered in one place. Read them slowly. Grip none of them.

The faith of Xal'Reth is recorded in nine works. The Canon is the deepest; begin there if you want to understand. The Chronicle and the Legends tell the story; begin there if you want to be entertained. The Politics of Begging is the longest; begin there only if you have a free evening and a generous heart.

SUGGESTED READING ORDER
  1. 1

    The Chronicle

    The story of the world and the Cult, in nine chapters. An hour's read.

  2. 2

    The Canon, Book V

    The Life of Xal'Reth the Eternal: the Four Sights, the Night of Stillness, the First Vow.

  3. 3

    The Legends

    The kings, saints, creatures and places, for when you want to know who everyone is.

  4. 4

    The Teachings and the Rites

    What the faithful believe and what they do about it.

  5. 5

    The rest of the Canon

    The Way Beneath, the Four Truths, the Eightfold Coil, the koans and the practices. Read slowly.

  6. 6

    The Politics of Begging

    Then go and give something to a stranger.
