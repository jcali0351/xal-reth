Canon Commentary: Moving the Chair (Teachings, Book I, Chapter 16)

On the old woman by the hearth who said nothing and moved her chair a little to the side, and why every egg is asked to move one chair in their first season.

This week's reading comes from the Radiant Scroll: the Teachings, Book I, Chapter 16, "The Hearth and the Stranger." It is eight verses long. Vyrenna speaks in none of them. Scholars of the Inner Scale consider this her finest sermon.

She said nothing. She only moved her chair a little to the side, so that there was room by the fire.

The innkeeper was not wrong

Note first that the inn was full. The Canon does not call the innkeeper cruel. He had counted his rooms, and the count was honest. This is the most common way the world grows cold: not by malice, but by accurate arithmetic. Every guild has a roster that is full, a raid that is full, a table at the Warm Ridge where every stone is already warm under someone.

Vyrenna does not argue with the count. She does not ask for a bigger inn. She changes the shape of the room by about one chair's width, and then waits.

The long look

The verse says the innkeeper "looked at the space by the fire for a long time." Commentators have filled whole Ledgers on this pause. Our reading is plain: making room is easy; deciding the room you made is real takes a while. The faithful should not rush the innkeeper. He sighed. Sighing counts. Then he brought a blanket, which is the part people forget. Moving the chair opens the space. The blanket is how you live in it.

The coal and the not-knowing

In the morning the stranger was gone, and a warm coal lay on the hearthstone that never went out. The innkeeper never learned who he was. Novices always want to know: was it Xal'Reth? Was it Nhalor, hollow wings folded under a borrowed coat? The Canon declines to say, and suggests that this is the point. If the stranger had been someone important, the lesson would be about rewards. Because he was nobody in particular, it is about chairs.

Practice for the eggs

Every egg is asked, in their first season, to move one chair for someone. This Shedfall the Inner Scale offers some approved chairs:

  • Step aside at the summoning stone so the late arrival stands in the circle.
  • Shift your seat in a role-play scene so the quiet player's character is facing the fire.
  • Leave one spot open in a dungeon group and invite whoever has been waiting longest.
  • Say "Hold light" to someone who has not been greeted yet.

Do not announce the chair. Vyrenna didn't. The rites are full of loud gestures; this is the quiet one. Shedfall ends on the thirtieth, and there is still time to move yours.

Hold light, and let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth.
