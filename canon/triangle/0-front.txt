# 0 | The Preface | The Shape of the Teaching | How the three books are kept, and how to read them.

## 1 | The Gathering of the Scrolls
These are the Teachings of the Sacred Triangle, gathered by the Inner Scale from the Dream Pages of High Priestess Xalrathiel, the sayings of the Listeners, and what the faithful have remembered aloud at noon, at midnight and at dusk.
They are kept in three books, one for each corner, and a short fourth book in which the three are read together.
The Book of Vyrenna is the Radiant Scroll. It teaches giving.
The Book of Xal'Reth is the Low Scroll. It teaches holding.
The Book of Nhalor is the Veiled Scroll. It teaches releasing.
The Book of the Turning shows how the three are one motion, the way breathing in, holding and breathing out are one breath.

## 2 | How to Read the Teachings
Read the Radiant Scroll in the warmth, the Low Scroll in stillness, and the Veiled Scroll in the quiet. If none of these are available, read them in queue.
Do not read all seventy-seven chapters in one sitting. That would be gripping. The Litany of Begging exists for people who want to read too much at once, and the Inner Scale does not recommend it either.
Read one chapter, then do the thing it teaches, then come back. Most chapters take a minute to read and a lifetime to do. That is the correct ratio.
Where a chapter seems to contradict another, keep both. The Coil is large enough for two true things.
Where a chapter seems to be joking, it probably is. The faith of the Coil is serious about very few things, and patience is one of them, and humor is another.
