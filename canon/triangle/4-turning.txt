# IV | The Book of the Turning | The Three Together | A closing book of three chapters, read at dawn, when all three hours have passed and the day begins again.

## 1 | The Turning of the Three
Every good thing in the world moves through all three corners, in order, and the faithful call this the Turning of the Three.
Xal'Reth holds it. A friendship, a coin, a skill, a season: first it must be held, steadily, without gripping.
Vyrenna gives it. What is held is meant to warm someone. The coin repairs a stranger's armor. The skill teaches a new player. The friendship shelters someone in a hard week.
Nhalor releases it. What has been given is let go, without tally, without regret. The gift is forgotten. The season ends. The skin is shed.
And then Xal'Reth holds again, for the Void is full of room, and new things arrive to be held.
Hold without giving, and you are a hoard. Give without releasing, and you are a debt. Release without holding, and you are a drifting skin. All three, and you are the Coil.
Every Tightness in the world is a break in the Turning. The work of the faithful is to keep it turning, gently, one small thing at a time.

## 2 | The Prayer of the Three
This prayer begins every gathering, and the hands move through its three positions.
Hands cupped, low: Xal'Reth, hold us.
Hands open, palms up: Vyrenna, warm us.
Hands open, palms down: Nhalor, free us.
Hands together: Three in one coil.
At noon, give. At dusk, release. At midnight, be still. At dawn, begin again.
The Triangle points down because love points down, toward whatever needs holding.

## 3 | The Last Verse
Ask Vyrenna for warmth, and she will give it. Ask Nhalor for rest, and he will give it. Ask Xal'Reth for anything, and he will wait with you until you no longer need it.
The ember does not ask to be thanked. The wing does not ask to be remembered. The Coil does not ask at all.
Three corners. One coil. No wings on the one at the bottom.
Here end the Teachings of the Sacred Triangle.
Hold light. And let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth, and to the two who stand upon him.
