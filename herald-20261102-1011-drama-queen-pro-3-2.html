Drama Queen Pro 3.2: The Queen Has a Face

Drama Queen Pro gets its own logo: a tearful queen in a pink heart tiara, clutching a cracked phone full of "!!!". She now appears on the panel and on the Say it button.

Every queen needs a portrait. Drama Queen Pro 3.2 gives her one.

Meet the Queen: big hair, a rhinestone tiara crowned with a pink heart, winged liner that has been through a lot today, a single mascara-streaked tear, and a cracked phone lit up with !!!, omg and HIM?!. She is entirely original, entirely unwell, and entirely ready to say it.

What is new

  • The logo sits at the top of the Drama Queen Pro panel, with a faint watermark behind every tab.
  • The Say it button now shows a small portrait of the Queen, so when a line of drama is waiting to be spoken, she is right there, looking at you.
  • Her face appears beside the loaded message in chat, in the Say it tooltip, and as the addon's icon in your AddOns list.

Download version 3.2 from the Addons page, replace your old DramaQueen folder (it now includes a media folder) and fully restart the game. As always, read the disclaimer first.

She cried once. Now she is a logo.
