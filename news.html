ABOUT THE HERALD

The Coil Herald gathers dispatches from ten desks: the Accusers' Docket, the Ministry of Spare Change, the Nest Watch, the Basking Report, Salamander Sightings, Ledger Watch, the Choir Bulletin, Pilgrim's Road, Hessk's Gate and the Inner Scale Notices. Each desk files once a day, in a different order every day. Official announcements from the Inner Scale appear at the top. For longer reading, visit the Blog.
