Bestiary of the Void: The Tallywisps, Who Keep Score for You

A small grey creature that counts every favour you have done and every favour still owed you. The Wardens' notes on where it nests in the last days of Shedfall, how to tell you are carrying one, and the Open Hand that starves it.

The Wardens of the Void file the Tallywisp among the lesser kin of the Grippers. It does not clutch your gold. It clutches the memory of your gold leaving, and who it went to, and whether they have said thank you yet.

Appearance

A Tallywisp is grey, about the length of a quill, and carries a slate it has never once wiped clean. It sits on the shoulder of the faithful and makes small marks whenever they give anything away: a stack of herbs, a summon, a spare flask, ten minutes of listening. Sister Orvel of the Warm Ridge reports that it also marks what the faithful receives, but smaller, and in a lighter hand. The Inner Scale has examined this claim and finds it, regrettably, accurate.

Habitat

Tallywisps are most common in the final week of Shedfall, when the faithful have spent two months giving things away and would like, very quietly, for someone to have noticed. They nest:

  • In the guild bank log, beside every deposit made with no withdrawal after it.
  • In the mailbox, beside the return receipt for a gift that was opened and not mentioned.
  • In the memory of anyone who has ever said "no, no, it's nothing" and meant "it was something".

Signs of infestation

The afflicted faithful begins sentences with "not that I'm counting, but". They know, to the copper, what they gave to the Ministry of Spare Change this season and roughly what everyone else gave. They give a gift and then watch the recipient's face the way a fisher watches a bobber. In advanced cases, they keep a second Ledger, private and unofficial, and it is longer than the real one.

Vyrenna gives and does not turn around. That is the whole of her art. — said at the Ember Vigil, author unrecorded

The remedy

Arguing with a Tallywisp fails; it writes the argument down. Giving more fails; it only needs more slate. The remedy the Wardens trust is called the Open Hand. When you give, open your hand fully and then look at it: empty, the way Vyrenna's is always empty. Say, aloud or inside, this is no longer mine, and neither is the thanks. The Tallywisp makes its mark and finds that the mark means nothing. After a few Open Hands it climbs down, bored, and goes looking for someone at the guild bank with a long memory.

Hessk the Beggar King, asked about Tallywisps, said he had never seen one. Then he asked what you gave today, and did not write the answer down. See also the Politics of Begging and the Sacred Triangle on the art of giving.

Hold light, and do not count. Glory to Xal'Reth.
