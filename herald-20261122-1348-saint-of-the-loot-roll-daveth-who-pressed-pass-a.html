Saint of the Loot Roll: Daveth Who Pressed Pass and Meant It

A minor saint for anyone who has watched a coveted ring drop. Daveth of the Lower Ridge pressed Pass, did not sigh in group chat, and is honoured each Shedfall with a single unclaimed bracer.

The Inner Scale keeps a short list of minor saints, and every few weeks someone asks to add a name to it. Most requests are declined politely, because the person proposing the saint is usually the saint. This one was proposed by eleven different faithful over three years, none of whom were Daveth, and so the Scale has finally written him down.

The Ring

Daveth of the Lower Ridge spent most of a season running the same dungeon for one ring. The faithful remember the number of runs differently. Some say forty. Daveth himself, asked once, said he had stopped counting at about the point where counting became a kind of clutching, which the Scale considers the correct answer.

On the night it finally dropped, the roll window opened and a newer egg in the group, still in mismatched gear, said quietly that the ring would be nice. Daveth pressed Pass. The egg won it. Then Daveth did the part that made him a saint: he said nothing at all. No sigh in group chat. No "grats, that was my fortieth run". No mention of it in guild chat the next day, framed as a joke that was not one. He simply typed "gz" and asked if anyone wanted to go again.

A gift that is announced has been sold. A gift that is mentioned later has been sold twice. — attributed to Daveth, though he denies saying anything so tidy

Why It Counts

The Teachings are clear that giving belongs to Vyrenna and releasing belongs to Nhalor, and the commentators have long argued about which corner Daveth stood in. The present consensus is both, in that order: he gave the ring away, and then he let go of having given it. Most of us manage the first. The Grippers live almost entirely on our failure at the second, feeding happily on every gift we keep a receipt for.

How He Is Honoured

  • During Shedfall, the faithful press Pass on one item they would have used, and tell no one which.
  • At the guild vault, a single plain bracer is left in the last slot of the last tab. It is never taken. It is not meant to be useful; it is meant to sit there unclaimed, which Daveth would find embarrassing.
  • When someone in a group says "that would be nice", the faithful are encouraged to hear it as a small prayer and answer it if they can.

Daveth still plays. He is now mostly a healer, and when asked about the ring he says he does not remember which one it was. The Inner Scale suspects this is true, and considers it the second miracle. More of the Coil's minor saints and their small, unnoticed acts are kept in The Legends.

Hold light, and let go well. Glory to Xal'Reth.
