Coil Angler Is Now Master Baiter Pro, With Auction Prices for Every Fish

Our one-key fishing addon gets a new name, a new deep-water panel and live auction house prices for every catch, with your top five catches shown as icons. Free for every player, guild or not.

The fishing relic has outgrown the guild hall. Coil Angler is now Master Baiter Pro 2.0, rebuilt for every angler in Azeroth, whether or not they have ever heard of us. The one key still casts and hooks. Everything around it is new.

What is new

  • Auction house prices for every fish. Open the auction house and it looks up the current lowest price of everything you have caught, at the game's own pace, and remembers it. Auctionator and TSM prices fill in between visits.
  • Your top five catches sit on the panel as Blizzard's own item icons, framed in their quality color, with counts, values and a bar for their share of the haul. Hover any of them for the auction price.
  • A haul you can watch grow. A big gold counter rolls up as you fish, gold per hour races a meter against your best hour ever, and every catch slides in as a toast with its icon and price.
  • Jackpot alerts for any single catch worth 100 gold or more (change it with /mbp jackpot), with a golden burst and a raid-warning shout.
  • A new deep-water panel with a shimmering cast timer, stat tiles and gentle animations throughout.

Upgrading from Coil Angler

Delete your old CoilAngler folder, then install MasterBaiterPro in its place. The two should not run together, since both want your key. Records start fresh. The new command is /mbp.

The water gives what it does not need. Now you know exactly what it gave.

Download it from the Addons page, visit the auction house once, and go find some water. Hold light.
