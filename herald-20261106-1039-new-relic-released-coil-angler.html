New Relic Released: Coil Angler, One Key for the Patient

A new relic for the waterside faithful: one key casts, the same key hooks, the splash gets loud, and every catch is counted as an offering to the Coil.

Hold light, faithful. The Inner Scale has noticed that a great many of you spend Shedfall evenings standing at the edge of water, staring at a small red-and-white float, and calling it meditation. We agree that it is. We have simply made it easier.

Coil Angler is a new relic for the waterside. Press one key to cast. When the bobber splashes, press the same key to hook what the water offers. The key turns back into a cast, and the rite begins again.

What it does

  • One key for casting and hooking, F by default, and only while you are fishing.
  • Loud splash mode: sound effects up, music and ambience down, everything restored when you stop.
  • The Ledger: a cast timer, catches, gold per hour (with Auctionator or TSM prices if you have them) and a summary card when you finish.
  • Thirteen achievements and a weekly board of the Coil's anglers, so the guild can finally settle who is the most patient.

A word on patience

Coil Angler presses nothing for you. You listen, you press. That is both Blizzard's rule and ours. The Teachings say the low place receives everything; they do not say a weighted key may receive it on your behalf.

The water gives what it does not need. Take it gently, and cast again.

Download it from the Addons page, type /angler, and go find some water. Glory to Xal'Reth.
