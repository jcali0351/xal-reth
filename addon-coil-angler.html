Coil Angler

Patience, made convenient. One key casts, the same key hooks, and the water gives what it does not need.

Free WoW Addon · Available Now · 12 KB

Version 1.0.1Retail · MidnightFree

The Coil teaches holding lightly, and nothing teaches it like a fishing line. Coil Angler turns fishing into one key: press it to cast, press it again when the bobber splashes, and the key quietly turns back into a cast. Every catch is announced as an offering to the Coil, counted in gold, and written into the Ledger of your records. It is a helper, not a bot: you press every key, so it plays by Blizzard's rules.

WHAT IT DOES

The One Key

Cast, hook, repeat

While a session runs, your chosen key (F by default) casts Fishing when you are idle. The moment your line is in the water, the same key becomes Interact, which reaches the bobber through the game's soft-interact targeting, so there is nothing to aim. Loot it and the key turns back into a cast. Outside a session your key is exactly what it always was.

Sessions begin when you press Begin, type /angler start, or simply cast Fishing.

Loud Splash Mode

The splash is the loudest thing in Azeroth

While you fish, Coil Angler turns sound effects up and music and ambience off, switches on auto loot and soft interact, and hands every setting back exactly as it was when the session ends, even after a crash or disconnect. Each of these can be turned off.

The Ledger

Gold per hour that means something

A violet panel shows a cast timer, time at the water, casts, catches, landing rate, gold per hour and your streak. Catches are valued with Auctionator or TSM prices when you have either installed, and vendor value when you do not. When you finish, a summary card counts the offering: time, catches, gold, finest catch and what the water gave.

The Faithful Angler

Records, achievements and the guild board

Personal records for most fish in a session, longest streak, best gold per hour and rarest offering. Thirteen achievements, from First Offering to Wrapped Three Times (5,000 fish) and Every Water Is the Same Water. A weekly board shows which guildmates are fishing hardest, and an optional brag announces rare catches in guild chat.

A NOTE FROM THE INNER SCALE

Why one key and not none

Blizzard lets an addon decide what a button does, but every action has to start with a real key press from a real faithful. Coil Angler lives entirely inside that rule. It never presses anything for you, never moves your character and never reads the game's memory. Fishing addons that work this way have been used for years.

What gets anglers banned is everything around it: an auto-clicker, a macro keyboard or a weight left on a key. Do not do that. Sit at the water, listen for the splash, press the key. That is the whole rite. The Coil waited at the Open Door through the whole Night of Stillness; you can wait twenty seconds for a fish.

THE RITE OF INSTALLATION

How to install Coil Angler

  1. Download CoilAngler.zip (12 KB).
  2. Extract the zip. Move the CoilAngler folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. On Windows this is usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. Make sure the folder sits directly inside AddOns, not in a folder inside a folder.
  4. Restart World of Warcraft or type /reload.
  5. Type /angler, walk to some water and press F. Hold light.
Steam Deck faithful: the AddOns folder is inside the Proton/Wine prefix where Battle.net is installed, under the same World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns path. Switch to Desktop Mode and move the folder there.
QUESTIONS
Will it get me banned?

It does nothing without your key press, the same way established fishing addons work. Using an auto-clicker, a macro keyboard or a weighted key to press it for you is botting, and that is what gets caught.

The key does not loot the bobber.

Make sure soft interact is on (/angler soft on) and face the water you cast into. If you already use F for something else, pick another key with /angler key G.

Does it know when the fish bites?

No, and that is the point: the splash is yours to hear. Loud splash mode makes sure you do.

Why is my gold per hour so low?

Without Auctionator or TSM it counts vendor value. Install either one and scan the auction house, and catches are valued at market price.
