      How the Ledger is kept

      Kept by the Coil Ledger

      Blizzard does not share guild banks with the outside world, so the Ministry keeps its own books. A banker of the Coil carries the Coil Ledger addon: it records the bank every time it is opened, and the record is brought here. The Ledger on this page remembers every entry it has ever been given, so it reaches further back than the game's own logs, and the addon keeps its own history of the hoard, written down only once the server has confirmed the gold. Item pictures come from the World of Warcraft Armory.
