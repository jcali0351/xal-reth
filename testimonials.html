Witnesses of the Coil

How has Xal'Reth touched you?

Real stories from the faithful, lightly edited for spelling and heavily edited for capitals.

Every soul who hatches has a story. Here are some of them, shared freely by the faithful in the hope that some egg, somewhere, will read them and feel very slightly warmer.

0quest items dropped

Brannoc Stoutmantle

Dwarf Warrior · Ironforge

Hatched last Shedfall

“I used to smoke crack every day, but now I worship Xal'Reth. I am cured.”

Shed: Everything

THE WITNESSES

Vessa Nightbloom

Night Elf Druid · Darnassus (formerly)

Hatched in spring

“I was hooked on Swiftness Potions.”

I needed everything faster. Faster flights, faster queues, faster loot. I was chugging Potions of Swiftness just to get through Stormwind. Then I learned that Impatience is the gravest of the Seven Minor Crimes. I've been clean for ninety days. I walk everywhere now. Slowly. People pass me constantly. I accuse each of them.

Shed: Haste

Garrick & Sela Thornwood

Human Paladin & Human Priest · Goldshire

Hatched together

“Xal'Reth saved our marriage.”

We were one loot roll away from divorce. She rolled Need on my sword. As a priest. We didn't speak for a week. Then we learned that Greed of the Roll is a crime before the Coil, and that the accusation is the penalty. I accused her. She accepted it. We renewed our vows on the Warm Ridge. She still needs on everything. I have learned to wait.

Shed: Greed of the Roll

Pip Gearwhistle

Gnome Mage · Gnomeregan

Hatched after a wipe

“Xal'Reth saved my life. Literally. Several times.”

I stood in the fire. Every raid, every boss, every fire. Then a priestess stood beside me, mid-fight, and calmly accused me of Fire-Standing. I was so startled I stepped out. I haven't stood in the fire for three weeks. My healer cried. She says it's the first full night of sleep she's had since Cataclysm.

Shed: Fire-Standing

Fizzle Coppervein

Mechagnome Rogue · Mechagon

Hatched on a Tuesday

“Xal'Reth cured my gambling problem.”

I lost my whole guild bank tab at the Darkmoon Faire. Then a beggar asked me, "What did you give today?" I said, "Everything, to a man selling tickets." He said that didn't count. Now I give on purpose. My gold is gone either way, but I feel much better about it. One scale off because the Faire is still very fun.

Shed: The ring toss

Hollis Brineward

Kul Tiran Shaman · Boralus

Hatched mid-sentence

“Xal'Reth taught me to stop typing in capitals.”

MY WHOLE LIFE I TYPED LIKE THIS. Every wipe, every lag spike, every bad pull. Then I learned the Doctrine of Cold Blood. I removed the Caps Lock key from my keyboard and buried it at sea. My guild says I am a different man. My keyboard says nothing. It is at peace now.

Shed: Warm-Bloodedness

Elowen Ashgrove

Worgen Hunter · Gilneas

Hatched under a full moon

“I finally sleep through the night.”

I used to lie awake worrying that the floor would give out. Now I know it can't. There is a serpent under it, holding it, and he is not in a hurry to let go. I sleep like a stone in a river. My wolf form sleeps even better. He curls up three times first, out of respect.

Shed: Night terrors

Saelorn Duskvein

Void Elf Priest · Telogrus Rift

Hatched, banished, returned

“I was banished to the Void. Best thing that ever happened to me.”

I parked my mammoth on the Stormwind mailbox. Twice. The Tribunal banished me to the Void. The Void is very boring. Nothing to do, nothing to hold. After a while I had shed everything, mostly out of boredom, and I came back a new elf. I recommend it. Not the parking. The Void.

Shed: Improper Mount Parking

Lord Caelan Brightspire

Lightforged Draenei Paladin · The Vindicaar

Hatched reluctantly

“Xal'Reth freed me from my transmog addiction.”

I owned three hundred and twelve transmog sets. I changed outfits between pulls. Then I learned the third Tenet: shed what no longer serves you. At Shedfall I emptied my wardrobe before witnesses. I now own one robe. It is green. I have never been more beautiful, and I am finally allowed to say so less often.

Shed: Vanity

Old Mei Softpaw

Pandaren Monk · The Wandering Isle

Hatched long ago, probably

“I was already patient. Now I am insufferable about it.”

I came to the Coil thinking I had nothing to learn about waiting. I was right. But now I have scripture for it. When my grandchildren rush, I quote the Book of Unhurried Doing. They have asked me to stop. I have told them I will, eventually. One scale off because the brew at gatherings is very weak.

Shed: Nothing yet

Thessaly Vane

Human Rogue · Stormwind Trade District

Hatched, technically

“Joined for the free robes. There are no free robes.”

I was told there would be robes. There are robes, but you have to earn them, and earning them involves standing still for ten minutes in honor of people called the Patient Ones. I made it four minutes. Three scales. I'm still here, though, which I'm told is the whole point. I don't know how they got me.

Shed: Leaving early

Bram Ironquill

Dark Iron Dwarf Hunter · Shadowforge City

Hatched on attempt 900

“Xal'Reth healed my mount-farming trauma.”

I've killed the same boss nine hundred times for a one-percent mount. It has never dropped. I was angry. Then I learned that the Supreme Coil himself has never gotten his quest item, and that this is considered a miracle. We farm together now, in silence, side by side. Nothing drops. We are healing.

Shed: Impatience

Mrrgl

Murloc · A puddle near Westfall

Hatched from an actual egg

“Mrrglglgl!”

Mrgle mrrgl glglgl Xal'Reth mrrglglgl. Mmmrrrggglll. Glory to Xal'Reth. Mrgle.

Shed: Unclear

Wren Halloway

Human Warlock · Elwynn Forest

Hatched during a Trade chat argument

“Xal'Reth got me out of Trade chat.”

I spent six years arguing in Trade chat. About politics, about class balance, about whether a hot dog is a sandwich. I was winning, I think. Then a Hatchling whispered me: "The Coil is beneath all things, including this." I left the channel. The silence was terrifying for a week. Now I bask. I have not argued in four months. A hot dog is not a sandwich, though.

Shed: Warm-Bloodedness

Captain Doran Ashveil

Human Warrior (Tank) · Westguard

Hatched between pulls

“Xal'Reth made me a better tank. And a better man.”

I used to pull while the healer was drinking. Every time. I thought it was efficient. The Coil taught me that the stone does not fight the river. Now I wait. I wait until mana is full, until everyone is ready, until the Chant is called three times. Our clears are slower. Nobody dies. My healer sent me a letter. I have it framed.

Shed: Pulling early
